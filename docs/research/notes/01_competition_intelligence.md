## Q1. Rules digest: Tech Startup Challenger 2027 (from `D:\tech_staruptdtu2627\TheLeStartup (2).pdf`)

### Takeaway
TSC 2027 runs in three rounds: Idea, then PoC/Prototype/MVP, then Final pitch. It is open to students at universities in southern Vietnam, in teams of at most 5. Products with Agentic AI get an explicit advantage. Round 1 cuts to the top 16 and Round 2 to the top 7. Pitching day is Saturday 27/02/2027. At pitching day, a live audience vote at the poster exhibition counts for **30% of each finalist's final-round score**. First prize is only 5,000,000 VND, so the main value is the title and sponsor gifts.

### Cited Findings
**Eligibility and teams**
- Eligible: (1) students of TDTU's Faculty of IT (CNTT); (2) students of other faculties with an IT-product startup idea, who may join; (3) students currently enrolled at universities in the southern region ("các trường đại học ở khu vực phía nam") — [Rules 2027 PDF, p.1](file:///D:/tech_staruptdtu2627/TheLeStartup%20%282%29.pdf)
- Team size is **no more than 5 students**. **More than 20%** of members must be Faculty-of-IT students "in case the team includes TDTU students". Teams may mix faculties. The rules recommend covering technology, operations, finance and marketing roles. The registration link and entry kit will be published on official TDTU and Faculty-of-IT channels — [Rules 2027 PDF, p.1](file:///D:/tech_staruptdtu2627/TheLeStartup%20%282%29.pdf)

**The 5 allowed domains, and the Agentic AI advantage**
- Ideas must create an IT product serving one of five domains: (1) Industry and product manufacturing (Công nghiệp, chế tạo sản phẩm); (2) Education, tourism, services, finance; (3) Health, healthcare, beauty technology; (4) Social-impact business (Kinh doanh tạo tác động xã hội); (5) Agriculture, forestry, fisheries — [Rules 2027 PDF, p.1](file:///D:/tech_staruptdtu2627/TheLeStartup%20%282%29.pdf)
- "BTC khuyến khích các đội thi ứng dụng Agentic AI vào sản phẩm. Các sản phẩm có Agentic AI sẽ có lợi thế hơn trong cuộc thi." The evaluation section repeats it: "Sản phẩm có tích hợp Agentic AI là một lợi thế trong cuộc thi." — [Rules 2027 PDF, pp.1 & 3](file:///D:/tech_staruptdtu2627/TheLeStartup%20%282%29.pdf)

**Entry rules**
- The product must **never have won the top prize ("giải cao nhất") in any startup competition at faculty level or above**. It must not be subject to a copyright dispute. Teams are responsible for the copyright of any resources they use. Copying leads to immediate disqualification — [Rules 2027 PDF, p.1](file:///D:/tech_staruptdtu2627/TheLeStartup%20%282%29.pdf)
- Documents must be in Vietnamese, Times New Roman size 14, A4, using the organisers' (BTC) template. "Correct presentation format" is itself a Round 1 criterion — [Rules 2027 PDF, pp.1 & 3](file:///D:/tech_staruptdtu2627/TheLeStartup%20%282%29.pdf)

**Timeline**
- Registration: per the accompanying announcement. Round 1 (Idea): **05/10/2026 – 15/11/2026**; judged 16–22/11/2026. Round 2 (Build): **23/11/2026 – 10/01/2027**; judged 11–17/01/2027. Final round: **18/01/2027 – 21/02/2027**. Pitching day: **13:30–18:00, 27/02/2027, Hall 10F, TDTU**. The schedule may change — [Rules 2027 PDF, p.2](file:///D:/tech_staruptdtu2627/TheLeStartup%20%282%29.pdf)
- Inconsistency: the PDF header is dated "TP. Hồ Chí Minh, ngày 18 tháng 9 năm 2027", but every round falls in Oct 2026 – Feb 2027 — [Rules 2027 PDF, p.1](file:///D:/tech_staruptdtu2627/TheLeStartup%20%282%29.pdf) (probably a typo for 18/9/2026)

**Deliverables per round**
- **Round 1 (Idea)** is judged on the submitted materials:
  - a project description (bản thuyết minh, BTC template) covering: Business Model Canvas; final-product description (necessity, feasibility…); resource plan (technology and staffing); commercial plan (customer analysis, rollout); financial plan (capital, upkeep, revenue); communications plan; team; idea; goals and value; market survey and competitive advantage; implementation plan; and any extras;
  - a **≤3-minute idea pitch video**;
  - optional images or video of the idea.
  - BTC runs one startup-skills seminar. **Top 16** advance — [Rules 2027 PDF, p.2](file:///D:/tech_staruptdtu2627/TheLeStartup%20%282%29.pdf)
- **Round 2 (Build)**: build a PoC, prototype or MVP and refine the Round 1 documents. Submit:
  - the improved Round 1 documents;
  - a **product poster (BTC template)**;
  - the working **PoC/Prototype/MVP**;
  - a **≤5-minute pitching video**.
  - BTC runs one seminar on "AI Agentic". **Top 7** advance — [Rules 2027 PDF, pp.2–3](file:///D:/tech_staruptdtu2627/TheLeStartup%20%282%29.pdf)
- **Final round**: improve the product using the Round 2 judges' feedback. Submit the finished project documents and the **pitch slides (10-minute pitch)** — [Rules 2027 PDF, p.3](file:///D:/tech_staruptdtu2627/TheLeStartup%20%282%29.pdf)

**Pitching day and the poster-exhibition vote**
- **All 16 Round 2 teams** exhibit their product posters, and **exhibition attendees vote directly**:
  - for the **7 finalists, the vote is 30% of the final-round score**;
  - among the **other 9 Round 2 teams**, the one with the most votes wins the "Đội thi được yêu thích nhất" (most-liked team) prize.
  - The 7 finalists pitch live to the final judges on 27/02/2027, and results are announced at the event — [Rules 2027 PDF, p.3](file:///D:/tech_staruptdtu2627/TheLeStartup%20%282%29.pdf)

**Judging criteria**
- **Round 1:**
  - novelty and creativity; feasibility; market fit; project potential;
  - necessity of the product for market demand; feasibility of production and business;
  - uniqueness and creativity versus existing products;
  - financial plan; communications plan ("giải pháp truyền thông độc đáo và khác biệt");
  - expected results (revenue, expected profit, growth potential, speed of development);
  - correct format — [Rules 2027 PDF, p.3](file:///D:/tech_staruptdtu2627/TheLeStartup%20%282%29.pdf)
- **Round 2** = Round 1 criteria **plus**:
  - ability to turn the project into a product;
  - **ability to deploy the product in reality immediately**;
  - production and business plan (product development, market expansion);
  - resources: team readiness, organisational structure, **key partners, fundraising approach**;
  - **risk assessment for real-world deployment** — [Rules 2027 PDF, pp.3–4](file:///D:/tech_staruptdtu2627/TheLeStartup%20%282%29.pdf)
- **Final** = Round 2 criteria **plus** presentation skill before the judges and the ability to persuade them — [Rules 2027 PDF, p.4](file:///D:/tech_staruptdtu2627/TheLeStartup%20%282%29.pdf)

**Prizes**
- One 1st prize: 5,000,000 VND + certificate. One 2nd: 3,000,000 VND. Two 3rd: 2,000,000 VND each. Three consolation prizes: 700,000 VND each. One most-liked team: 500,000 VND.
- Prize values may rise with enterprise sponsorship. Finalists also receive gifts from professional patrons ("Bảo trợ chuyên môn") and sponsors — [Rules 2027 PDF, p.4](file:///D:/tech_staruptdtu2627/TheLeStartup%20%282%29.pdf)

**Contacts**
- CLB học thuật ICON (ICON academic club), Faculty of IT, TDTU — email **icon.acaclub@it.tdt.edu.vn** (domain as printed in the PDF).
- **ThS. Dung Cẩm Quang**, Faculty of IT lecturer — **dungcamquang@tdtu.edu.vn** — [Rules 2027 PDF, p.4](file:///D:/tech_staruptdtu2627/TheLeStartup%20%282%29.pdf)

### Inferences
- **Every finalist wins something:** 1 + 1 + 2 + 3 = 7 prizes for 7 finalists. Making the top 7 guarantees at least a consolation prize. Winning 1st means beating 6 teams, with 30% of the score decided by the audience.
- **The audience vote is decisive.** The rules do not say how votes become the 30% (for example, relative to the most-voted team). A large vote lead could still offset a gap of several judge points. Ask BTC how votes are normalised and who may vote.
- **The ">20% IT students" rule** means a 5-member team that includes TDTU students needs at least 2 IT students (1 of 5 is exactly 20%, not "more than"). A mostly-IT team meets this easily.
- **Stricter than 2023:** the 2023 rules barred only products that had won the top prize at **city level or above**; 2027 bars any top prize at **faculty level or above** (see Q2). Do not reuse a project that has already won 1st anywhere at faculty level or above.
  - Risk: TDTU's school-wide startup contest runs on an overlapping schedule (Oct–Jan; see Q3). Winning its 1st prize before the TSC final could create an eligibility question. Confirm with ThS. Dung Cẩm Quang.
- **The schedule is tight:** about **42 days** from today (04/10/2026) to the Round 1 deadline (15/11/2026), and about **48 days** for the Round 2 MVP, poster and video window (23/11/2026–10/01/2027). The final-round improvement window (18/01–21/02/2027) likely overlaps the Lunar New Year (Tết) break of 2027 (believed to be early February; verify). This limits pilot or user testing then, so traction should be gathered in November–December 2026.
- **Agentic AI is weighted at every stage:** it is in the topic section, in the evaluation preamble, and in a dedicated Round 2 seminar. Judges will likely probe whether the product is truly agentic (multi-step planning, tool or API use, autonomous actions with human-in-the-loop safeguards) or just a chatbot wrapper.
- **The Round 1 rubric includes the communications plan.** A comms plan that doubles as the audience-vote campaign (Q5) serves both the Round 1 score and the 30% vote.
- **Round 2 criteria reward evidence of real deployment:** "immediate deployability", key partners, fundraising and a risk assessment. Pilot users or letters of intent and a risk register (including AI-specific risks) map directly onto these criteria.

### Gaps
- The registration announcement (link, deadline, entry-kit template) was not found online as of 04/10/2026. The PDF defers to "the announcement attached".
- How the 30% vote score is computed, who may vote (TDTU students only? external guests? one vote per person?) and the voting medium (paper, QR or Google Form) are not specified.
- Names of the 2027 judges and sponsors are not published.

### Inferences
- **Travel is a proven winner at TSC, but repetitive.** Two of three known 1st prizes went to AI travel assistants (2023, 2025). A third travel app would struggle on the Round 1 criterion "tính độc đáo… so với các sản phẩm đã có". If the team picks tourism, it needs a sharply different, agentic angle; otherwise it should avoid the category.
- **Recurring judges or sponsors are likely.** VTI Academy's Đỗ Đăng Khoa appears in 2023 (presenting 1st prize) and 2025 (judge). Faculty leadership (Lê Anh Cường, Lê Văn Vang, the Software Engineering department) judged in 2025 and led 2026. Expect technically literate questions on architecture, data and AI validity, plus industry questions on business model and go-to-market.
- **Mixed teams dominated in 2023.** All three 2023 winners included Business Administration or Finance members, and the only all-IT finalist (YS) did not place (small sample). The 2026 SBS business-school teams also placed. A mostly-IT team should still present clear business, finance and marketing roles; it could add one business or marketing student while keeping more than 20% IT.
- **Iterating across contests is normal and allowed.** Winners often polish a project across contests: CharityChain (District 7, 2nd) went on to TSC 3rd; Hodos went from a TDTU-wide consolation prize to TSC 1st; Q Ride Park grew out of a student research project. The 2027 rule only forbids projects that already won a top prize.
- **Faculty advisors matter.** Several prize projects had faculty advisors, and the 2027 contact person advised the likely Hodos project in 2024–25. Securing a lecturer advisor or mentor early is common practice among winners.
- **Live demo and Q&A are standard at the final.** The judges value "khả năng triển khai" (deployability) and a clear roadmap. A working agent demo plus real users or pilots is the likely winning formula.
- **The poster vote appears new in 2027.** The 2023 rules and the 2025/2026 final reports never mention a poster exhibition or audience vote, so there is no TSC precedent for vote dynamics.

### Inferences
Shared traits of winners, as inferred from the cited cases:
1. **A specific problem for a named segment** (eldercare at home; foreign tourists in HCMC; enterprises losing data to AI agents; deaf users; flood victims), not a generic "AI assistant".
2. **A complete, demo-able product.** NTTU's winner was praised for a "complete technical solution"; TSC 2026 included live demos; TSC 2027 Round 2 explicitly scores "immediate deployability".
3. **Social impact or safety framing.** Disaster and rescue, eldercare, accessibility and personal safety cluster heavily among 2026 prize-winners. Techfest explicitly rewards measurable impact.
4. **B2B / SaaS logic and monetisation clarity** (AIGuard, RentUp's SaaS approach, ThueDonGian). Sponsor-judges (TikTok for Business, Spiraledge, VTI Academy, Axon Active) evaluate commercial viability.
5. **Agentic AI is emerging but not yet saturated in the winners' circle.** AI-agent governance won NTTU; agentic "virtual staff" appeared in HCMUT's start-up track. A credible agentic product with guardrails and a live demo should stand out at TSC, especially since TSC explicitly rewards it.

Other observations:
- The TSC rubric and format clone the MOET/HCMC "Học sinh, sinh viên với ý tưởng khởi nghiệp" family. A strong TSC dossier can be reused for TDTU's school-wide contest and the MOET pipeline, but watch TSC's "no prior top prize" rule and the timing.
- Prize-winning student projects in Vietnam are often vetted by faculty advisors and refined across several contests; that is a sign of maturity that judges reward.

  - SV_STARTUP national winners for 2024 and 2025;

### Inferences
- **Avoid, or sharply differentiate:**
  - generic AI travel planners (two TSC wins already; a "third travel app" is hard to call novel);
  - AI study chatbots and tutors;
  - mental-health chatbots;
  - sign-language translators (now placing mostly 3rd);
  - plant / skin / X-ray disease classifiers;
  - face-recognition attendance;
  - smart parking;
  - blockchain charity;
  - generic customer-service or e-commerce chatbots.

  If the team keeps one of these, the differentiator must be an **agent that acts**: books, files, negotiates, monitors or reconciles across tools. It must be tied to a narrow paying customer and backed by pilot evidence.
- **Under-served spaces that also match TSC's 5 domains and Agentic-AI bonus:**
  - **domain 1 (industry / manufacturing):** agentic maintenance, QC or procurement copilots for small factories;
  - **domain 5 (aquaculture / fisheries, agriculture):** agents that monitor sensors or markets and act for farmers or co-ops;
  - **domain 2 (finance / services):** compliance and e-invoice / tax agents for Vietnam's millions of household businesses, the area ThueDonGian touched;
  - **domain 3:** beauty-tech, and care coordination for elderly or chronic patients;
  - **domain 4:** AI-agent safety for SMEs, scam defence for the elderly.
- **The 2026 social-impact clusters** (eldercare, disaster response, personal safety, scam defence) are resonating with judges. Combining one with real agentic autonomy and a B2B2C revenue model fits both the "Kinh doanh tạo tác động xã hội" domain and the Round 2 "deploy immediately" criterion.

## Q5. Tactics that win audience votes at Vietnamese university poster exhibitions / startup showcases

### Takeaway
There is little direct evidence about what wins audience votes in Vietnamese contests. Known facts:
- TSC 2027's vote is **in person, by exhibition attendees, on a Saturday afternoon**, with a **BTC-mandated poster template**.
- The faculty's precedent is **per-project Google-Form "Bình chọn" voting** on its exhibition pages; the 2023 form requires Google sign-in.
- Vietnamese contests visibly reward **booths and videos**: "impressive booth" and "most-liked film" prizes, and pre-final product exhibitions.

The likely levers, all to be cleared with BTC, are: **turnout of supporters**, a **30-second interactive agent demo via QR code**, a **human story**, and a **pre-campaign that doubles as the Round 1 communications plan**.

### Inferences
Everything in this section is inference; none of these tactics was tested in a cited source.
- **Treat the 30% as a turnout game.** Voting is in person, and every TSC final so far has been on a **Saturday afternoon** (22/07/2023, 10/05/2025, 09/05/2026, 27/02/2027 — weekdays computed from the cited dates).
  - Recruit supporters to attend: classmates, club friends, pilot users, mentors.
  - Remind them via Zalo / Facebook groups.
  - Make the booth worth visiting.
- **Run the pre-campaign from Round 1.** Content can include short TikTok / Reels clips of the agent solving real tasks, user stories, and a countdown to 27/02. This builds name recognition before the event and serves as the "unique communications plan" scored in Round 1.
- **Make the Agentic AI tangible in 30 seconds.** Put a QR code on the poster or a standee. A visitor scans it and gives the agent a task (for example, "find and book…" or "check my…"). The agent completes it live or sends a result to the visitor's phone. This shows the agentic advantage to laypeople and creates a natural moment to ask for a vote.
- **Differentiate beyond the fixed poster template:** a looping demo video on a laptop or tablet; a physical prop (a sensor kit for agriculture or aquaculture ideas); a large one-line problem statement; a real beneficiary's testimonial (emotional storytelling).
- **Small giveaways or a mini-game** (stickers, candy, a "beat the agent" quiz) can increase dwell time, but **confirm with BTC that these and any vote solicitation are allowed**. Avoid anything resembling vote-buying, which could disqualify the team.
- **Staffing:** finalists must run the booth and pitch the same afternoon. Plan for 2–3 members at the booth while the pitchers prepare, and keep the booth staffed throughout.
- **Ask BTC early:**
  - Who may vote (TDTU students only? external guests? judges and sponsors?).
  - The medium (paper, QR, or Google Form with sign-in).
  - Whether there is one vote per person or multiple choices.
  - When voting closes.
  - How raw votes are scaled to the 30%.

  The tactics depend on these answers. If the vote uses Google sign-in restricted to TDTU accounts, external supporters will not count.

### Gaps
- No Vietnamese source measured the effect of QR interactions, mini-games, giveaways, social-media pre-campaigns or storytelling on audience votes at university showcases.
- No TSC precedent exists, since the vote appears new in 2027.
- The voting procedure, the eligible voters and how votes are scored are unpublished.
