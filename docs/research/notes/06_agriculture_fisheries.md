## Q1. EUDR: application dates as of late 2026, obligations, Vietnam's exposure and readiness, smallholder burden, existing solutions

### Takeaway
The EUDR applies from **30 Dec 2026** to large and medium operators, with no further delay expected, and from **30 Jun 2027** to micro and small operators. Vietnam is classified as **"low risk"**. EU importers therefore skip the formal risk assessment and risk mitigation steps (Art. 10/11), and only 1% of consignments are checked. They still need **plot-level geolocation for every plot**, a polygon for plots over 4 ha, and legality evidence for each shipment. For Vietnam, the hard problem is now smallholder data: about 60% of coffee households keep no consistent harvest records, only about 10% have plot-level data, and the same plot often appears in several buyers' systems. Detecting deforestation matters much less, because large-scale mapping found it to be rare.

### Cited Findings
**Dates and legal changes**
- The amending regulation is **Regulation (EU) 2025/2650**, published in the OJ on 23 Dec 2025. New application dates: medium and large operators/traders **30 Dec 2026**, micro and small operators **30 Jun 2027**. It also required the Commission to carry out a simplification review by 30 Apr 2026 — [Stibbe](https://www.stibbe.com/publications-and-insights/the-amended-eudr-what-has-changed-and-what-has-remained). The Council signed off the targeted revision on 18 Dec 2025 — [Consilium (search result)](https://www.consilium.europa.eu/en/press/press-releases/2025/12/18/deforestation-council-signs-off-targeted-revision-to-simplify-and-postpone-the-regulation/)
- Micro and small companies in the timber sector also start on 30 Dec 2026. Other micro and small companies start on 30 Jun 2027 — [Arendt](https://www.arendt.com/news-insights/news/eudr-simplification-review-published-what-the-2026-package-means-for-in-scope-companies/)
- The December 2025 amendment moved due-diligence responsibility to the **first operator placing a product on the EU market**. Downstream operators and traders now have lighter duties: they collect partner information and keep DDS reference numbers. **Micro or small primary operators (MSPOs)** submit a **one-time simplified declaration**, but must be established in a country classified as low risk under Art. 29 — [Arendt](https://www.arendt.com/news-insights/news/eudr-simplification-review-published-what-the-2026-package-means-for-in-scope-companies/)
- The simplified declaration still has to include the country of production and **geodata coordinates of the plots** — [Stibbe](https://www.stibbe.com/publications-and-insights/the-amended-eudr-what-has-changed-and-what-has-remained)
- Unchanged requirements: geolocation of plots, **polygons for plots > 4 ha** (non-cattle commodities), the **31 Dec 2020** deforestation cut-off, the legality requirement, and **5-year** record retention — [Stibbe](https://www.stibbe.com/publications-and-insights/the-amended-eudr-what-has-changed-and-what-has-remained)
- Legality has to be shown across **eight categories** of producer-country law: land-use rights, environment, forest management, third-party rights, labour, human rights, FPIC, and tax/anti-corruption. Mayer Brown says this in practice means auditing producers in the supply chain. Commissioner Jessika Roswall opposed reopening the regulation again — [Mayer Brown, Feb 2026](https://www.mayerbrown.com/en/insights/publications/2026/02/eu-regulation-on-deforestation-free-products-eudr-what-lies-ahead-in-2026)
- **Simplification package of 4 May 2026 (COM(2026) 191).** It contains a draft delegated act that **adds soluble/instant coffee** and certain palm-oil derivatives to scope and **removes** leather, retreaded tyres and some other items, with consultation until 1 Jun 2026. It also includes Information System upgrades: the system reopened in June 2026, DDS reference numbers can be grouped, and new operator roles were added. The Commission claims compliance costs fall by about 75% against the original framework, and it confirmed the 30 Dec 2026 date with no postponement — [HLC](https://www.hlc.com/en/publications/eu-deforestation-regulation-commission-publishes-simplification-package-ahead-of-december-2026); [EC report PDF (search result)](https://environment.ec.europa.eu/document/download/a3c5c3a0-232e-43c4-b0b8-1eecb1df45c7_en?filename=Report+from+the+Commission+to+the+Council+and+Parliament+on+the+EUDR.pdf)
- Repositories of producing-country legislation and certification schemes are planned "before the end of 2026" — [Arendt](https://www.arendt.com/news-insights/news/eudr-simplification-review-published-what-the-2026-package-means-for-in-scope-companies/)

**Low-risk status of Vietnam**
- Vietnam was classified **"low risk"** by Implementing Regulation (EU) 2025/1093, adopted 22 May 2025. Member-state authorities check **1%** of consignments from low-risk countries, against 3% for standard risk and 9% for high risk. Indonesia and Malaysia are standard risk. Thailand, the Philippines and Laos are low risk — [SGGP, 23 May 2025](https://en.sggp.org.vn/vietnam-achieves-low-risk-status-under-eu-deforestation-regulation-post117784.html)
- Operators sourcing from low-risk countries **still collect all Art. 9 information, including geolocation**, and still submit a **DDS for each shipment**. They are not required to carry out the Art. 10 risk assessment or Art. 11 mitigation unless they become aware of a risk. Coolset reports that from July 2026 the IS lets operators group several DDS reference numbers, and that the MSPO one-off declaration was formalised by "Implementing Regulation (EU) 2026/1565" (single source, not cross-checked) — [Coolset, updated Jul 2026](https://www.coolset.com/academy/eudr-due-diligence-for-low-risk-countries-what-changes-and-what-stays-the-same)

**Vietnam's exposure**
- In 2025, coffee exports to the EU were **US$3.63 bn** (40.7% of coffee exports; 666,300 t), out of total coffee exports of **US$8.92 bn**. The EU was the third-largest rubber market at **US$772.2 m**. The same article puts "rubber exports" at US$11.45 bn, which probably includes rubber products; I could not verify the scope. For timber, Vietnam has about 15 m ha of forest, more than 42% cover, about 5,000 processing enterprises, and exports to about 170 countries — [Vietstock/FILI, 26 Sep 2026](https://vietstock.vn/2026/09/eudr-mo-cuoc-dua-truy-xuat-nguon-goc-nong-san-viet-118-1486963.htm)
- Processed coffee exports passed **US$1 bn** for the first time in 2025 — [Koltiva, Jan 2026](https://www.koltiva.com/post/winning-in-the-eu-market-how-vietnam-s-us-8b-coffee-sector-can-transition-from-volume-to-verified). This matters because soluble coffee is proposed for addition to the EUDR scope ([HLC](https://www.hlc.com/en/publications/eu-deforestation-regulation-commission-publishes-simplification-package-ahead-of-december-2026)).
- 2026 coffee prices are falling. H1 2026 exports were 1.1 Mt and US$4.78 bn (volume +9.7%, value −14.4%) — [Công Thương, 12 Jul 2026](https://congthuong.vn/xuat-khau-ca-phe-sang-eu-lo-chong-lan-du-lieu-truoc-quy-dinh-eudr-465068.html). Over 8 months of 2026, exports were more than US$6 bn, with value down about 11.2% year on year — [Dân Việt, 2 Sep 2026](https://etime.danviet.vn/suc-bat-xuat-khau-nong-san-8-thang-sau-rieng-bung-no-hat-dieu-giu-gia-ca-phe-giam-sau-d1456193.html)

**Readiness: national database, pilots, partners**
- The forest and coffee-area database built by MARD, IDH and JDE Peet's was handed over on **17 Dec 2024** (background). It covered **4 pilot districts in Lâm Đồng and Đắk Lắk**, with Gia Lai joining later, and catalogued about **130,000 ha of forest and 136,000 ha of coffee**. Every garden plot received a **unified cadastral code** — [Nông nghiệp & Môi trường](https://nongnghiepmoitruong.vn/thi-diem-thanh-cong-he-thong-co-so-du-lieu-rung-va-vung-trong-ca-phe-d413763.html); [IDH (background)](https://idh.org/news/vietnams-coffee-sector-marks-a-landmark-step-towards-sustainability-launching-the-eudr-database-system-for-forest-and-coffee-growing-areas)
- As of September 2026, coffee geocoding pilots are running in Sơn La, Thái Nguyên, Đắk Lắk and Lâm Đồng. For rubber, about **305,000 ha (81% of VRG-managed area)** "basically meets EUDR" as of June 2026. For timber, only **25,500 ha** are piloting forest-zone codes. IDH, GIZ and JDE Peet's co-developed the coffee database — [Vietstock, 26 Sep 2026](https://vietstock.vn/2026/09/eudr-mo-cuoc-dua-truy-xuat-nguon-goc-nong-san-viet-118-1486963.htm)
- A **2025 survey by Forest Trends and Tavina** found that about **60% of coffee households keep no consistent harvest records** and only **about 10% have plot-level data**. Sustainable forest-management plans plus certification cost **50–200 m VND** — [Vietstock](https://vietstock.vn/2026/09/eudr-mo-cuoc-dua-truy-xuat-nguon-goc-nong-san-viet-118-1486963.htm)
- The **data-overlap problem**: the same plot is registered in several companies' certification programmes. Only about 30% of coffee area is certified, and the remaining **70% is a "blind zone"**. As of July 2026, **no unified national origin database** is operational. VICOFA Vice-Chair Thái Như Hiệp (CEO of Vĩnh Hiệp) asked local authorities to cross-verify overlaps before EUDR applies. Tô Xuân Phúc of Forest Trends called for state data infrastructure — [Công Thương, 12 Jul 2026](https://congthuong.vn/xuat-khau-ca-phe-sang-eu-lo-chong-lan-du-lieu-truoc-quy-dinh-eudr-465068.html)
- In Gia Lai there are about **107,400 ha of coffee**, more than half certified (Organic/RA/4C). **436 growing-area codes** cover about 13,000 ha of coffee and durian combined. Plan 331 aims to map all zones before end-2026, and full data coverage is estimated to need **about 2 years**. Vĩnh Hiệp links about 21,000 households and 20,000 ha, with the EU taking 45% of its exports. HTX Ia Púch is expanding coffee from 25 ha to 100 ha — [Người Đưa Tin, 27 Aug 2026](https://www.nguoiduatin.vn/ca-phe-gia-lai-chay-dua-voi-eudr-204260826211627817.htm)
- JDE Peet's and Enveritas mapped **1.8 million coffee plots in Vietnam** with AI on satellite imagery and found **fewer than 200** deforested after 2020. JDE described manual GPS boundary walking as "time consuming, unreliable, and costly" — [FI Global Insights, Jun 2024 (background)](https://insights.figlobal.com/sustainability/jde-peet-s-using-ai-and-advanced-satellite-imagery-to-make-coffee-deforestation-free)

**Existing solutions**
- Platforms used for EUDR include Beyco, Farmforce, Koltiva, Satelligence, Meridia Verify and Single.Earth. Vietnam is **Koltiva's third-largest coffee origin**. KoltiTrace offers producer and plot registration, GPS/polygon mapping and geospatial risk analysis — [Koltiva (search snippet)](https://www.koltiva.com/post/winning-in-the-eu-market-how-vietnam-s-us-8b-coffee-sector-can-transition-from-volume-to-verified)
- Koltiva reports 1,258,788 verified farm plots and 487,339 registered producers since 2013. It is unclear whether these figures are for Vietnam or global — [Koltiva](https://www.koltiva.com/post/winning-in-the-eu-market-how-vietnam-s-us-8b-coffee-sector-can-transition-from-volume-to-verified)
- TraceX markets GPS/polygon mapping, AI and satellite deforestation alerts, **automatic DDS generation per shipment** ("weeks to hours"), and a blockchain ledger for Vietnamese coffee exporters. No pricing is published — [TraceX](https://tracextech.com/eudr-exporter/eudr-vietnam-coffee-exporter/)
- Kanop offers AI and satellite monitoring of deforestation and land conversion for EUDR due diligence across coffee, cocoa, rubber, palm and soy — [Kanop (search result)](https://www.kanop.io/)
- One Vietnamese supplier claims that suppliers enrolled in geotraceability systems earn a **2–4% FOB premium** on EU-bound contracts. This is a vendor claim — [Tám Trình Coffee (search snippet)](https://export.tamtrinhcoffee.com/traceable-green-coffee-supply-chain/)

### Inferences
- For Vietnam, the EUDR problem is now about **data logistics** — collecting polygons and legality documents from hundreds of thousands of smallholders, removing duplicates, managing mass balance, and producing DDS/declaration packs. Deforestation analytics matter less. Low-risk status removes most of the risk-analysis workload but does not remove geolocation.
- The **30 Dec 2026** start date falls **inside Round 2 of the competition** (23 Nov 2026 – 10 Jan 2027), which makes for a strong "why now" in a pitch. The SME/MSPO date (30 Jun 2027) gives a second wave.
- Large exporters and VRG appear to be well served by Koltiva, Enveritas/JDE and IDH, and VRG reports 81% of its area as compliant. The underserved segments are likely **mid-size coffee exporters and cooperatives, rubber smallholders outside VRG, timber SMEs** (only 25,500 ha piloting zone codes), and the **newly in-scope soluble coffee**.

### Gaps
- I could not verify whether the EU Information System offers a public **API or sandbox** for third-party DDS submission, or what formats it requires (e.g., GeoJSON).
- I could not verify whether Vietnam's low-risk status was confirmed or changed in any **2026 benchmarking review**. Coolset mentions a "May 2026" country classification, but I did not verify the details.
- No published **prices** were found for Koltiva, Farmforce, Satelligence, TraceX or Kanop, and there is no reliable cost-per-farmer figure for EUDR compliance in Vietnam.
- I found no data on Vietnam's cocoa exposure (likely small) or on timber exports to the EU in 2025.

## Q2. Durian and fruit exports to China (2025–2026): testing, codes, values, traceability, struggles

### Takeaway
Durian is Vietnam's largest fruit export: **US$3.85 bn in 2025**, about **US$2.7 bn in the first 8 months of 2026** (+52%), and roughly 95% goes to China. China requires **100% testing for cadmium and Auramine O** on every lot. Since 2025, China has warned **403 planting-area codes and 240 packing houses**, and **167 codes and 99 packing houses** were suspended or revoked. Three new developments land in 2026: a **national durian traceability system** live nationwide from **1 Jul 2026**, **Decree 38/2026/NĐ-CP** on code management, and **Resolution 36/2026/NQ-CP**, which decentralises code issuance. In July 2026, a **Deputy Minister and a Deputy Director of the plant-protection department were arrested** over alleged trading of codes and test certificates. Together these create urgent demand for trustworthy compliance records that can be audited.

### Cited Findings
**Export values**
- Durian exports were **US$3.85 bn in 2025**, from about **200,000 ha** producing about **2.08 Mt**. The 2026 target is about **US$4 bn**. The government press conference named problems with cadmium and Auramine O, lab capacity, complex code procedures, a lack of cold storage and processing, and weak chain coordination. **Resolution 36/2026/NQ-CP** simplifies procedures and **decentralises issuance of planting and packing codes to commune and ward level**. A digital traceability system has run since **1 Jul 2026**. Deputy Minister Đặng Ngọc Điệp spoke — [VNA Chính sách & Cuộc sống, 3 Sep 2026](https://chinhsachcuocsong.vnanet.vn/hop-bao-chinh-phu-thao-go-kho-khan-phan-dau-dua-xuat-khau-sau-rieng-nam-2026-dat-khoang-4-ty-usd/95209.html). VietnamNet gives US$3.86 bn for 2025 ([VietnamNet](https://vietnamnet.vn/thu-truong-va-loat-can-bo-bi-bat-lo-goc-khuat-cap-ma-so-vung-trong-sau-rieng-2533152.html)).
- Over 8 months of 2026, durian exports were **US$2.7 bn (+52%)**. August 2026 alone was **US$956 m (+62%)**. China took US$2.58 bn over the 8 months (+59%). Exports to the US were US$18.3 m and to Korea US$3.4 m. Mid-September farm-gate prices were **50,000–60,000 VND/kg**, and a 2026 projection gives US$4.5–5 bn. Data from Vietnam Customs — [Thanh Niên, 30 Sep 2026](https://thanhnien.vn/xuat-khau-sau-rieng-mang-ve-27-ti-usd-sau-8-thang-185260930135134228.htm). This is consistent with 7-month durian exports of **US$1.77 bn (+47.1%)**, China taking more than 93%, and 8-month fruit and vegetable exports of **about US$5.87 bn (+21.8%)** — [Dân Việt, 2 Sep 2026](https://etime.danviet.vn/suc-bat-xuat-khau-nong-san-8-thang-sau-rieng-bung-no-hat-dieu-giu-gia-ca-phe-giam-sau-d1456193.html)
- **Conflicting figures.** One government-press summary gives "US$1.95 bn for the first 8 months" of 2026 ([VNA](https://chinhsachcuocsong.vnanet.vn/hop-bao-chinh-phu-thao-go-kho-khan-phan-dau-dua-xuat-khau-sau-rieng-nam-2026-dat-khoang-4-ty-usd/95209.html)). Báo Chính phủ gives "Q1 2026 about US$1.3 bn (+128%)" ([Báo Chính phủ, 26 May 2026](https://baochinhphu.vn/bai-2-go-nut-that-cadimi-tai-thiet-chuoi-sau-rieng-de-giu-thi-truong-xuat-khau-102260524220023812.htm)). Neither fits the Customs-based series. I would use Thanh Niên/Dân Việt.
- Durian made up 45% of national fruit and vegetable export value — [Báo Chính phủ, 26 May 2026](https://baochinhphu.vn/bai-2-go-nut-that-cadimi-tai-thiet-chuoi-sau-rieng-de-giu-thi-truong-xuat-khau-102260524220023812.htm)

**Testing: cadmium and Auramine O**
- China requires **100%** of China-bound durian lots to carry cadmium and Auramine O test certificates. In 2025, Đắk Lắk lost **55 planting codes and 61 packing-house codes** over cadmium. Only about **20% of about 150,000 ha** had codes at that time. The Đắk Lắk Durian Association (Chairman Vũ Đức Côn) asked for "red alert" zoning and soil remediation — [Tuổi Trẻ/PLO, 21 May 2025](https://tuoitre.vn/plo/ti-le-vang-o-khong-dat-bao-dong-do-doi-voi-vu-sau-rieng-2025-109850947.htm)
- **Lab bottleneck in May 2026.** 25 facilities were recognised, but only 16 of 21 cadmium labs and 19 of 22 Auramine O labs were working normally. HCMC had 15 labs, 13 of them functioning. **Hundreds of containers were backed up at the Dầu Giây hub in Đồng Nai** waiting for sampling. New lab licence applications faced a **6-month backlog**. The Deputy Minister called testing "one of the system's largest bottlenecks", and VINAFRUIT Secretary-General Đặng Phúc Nguyên also commented — [Dân Trí, 18 May 2026](https://dantri.com.vn/kinh-doanh/nut-that-kiem-dinh-sau-rieng-co-tien-van-kho-mo-phong-xet-nghiem-20260518081939827.htm)
- As of 26 Jun 2026, **37 labs** were accepted by GACC: 8 in the Red River Delta, 4 in the Central Highlands and South-Central Coast, **15 in HCMC**, and **10 in the Mekong Delta** (8 in Cần Thơ, 1 in Đồng Tháp, 1 in Cà Mau). **Lâm Đồng still lacks labs** — [Dân Việt, 7 Jul 2026](https://danviet.vn/bo-nnmt-cong-bo-danh-sach-37-co-so-kiem-nghiem-cadimi-vang-o-duoc-hai-quan-trung-quoc-chap-nhan-d1441332.html)
- Setting up a certified lab costs **about 20 bn VND**. In one week the farm-gate price fell more than 50%, with Ri6 at about 30,000 VND/kg against a production cost of about 35,000 VND/kg. When fruit from several orchards is consolidated into one container, **a single failed sample rejects the whole shipment**. Cadmium comes from decades of intensive fertiliser use. Article date not shown; context suggests 2025 — [Thương hiệu & Công luận](https://thuonghieucongluan.com.vn/nut-that-kiem-nghiem-cadimi-va-vang-o-khien-gia-sau-rieng-giam-manh-a316378.html)
- Test turnaround is "24–48 hours or longer" — [Tuổi Trẻ, 31 Jul 2026](https://tuoitre.vn/quy-trinh-cap-ma-vung-trong-giay-kiem-nghiem-sau-rieng-xuat-khau-sang-trung-quoc-the-nao-100260730182952725.htm)

**Codes**
- Planting-area codes require at least **10 ha**, so smallholders must join cooperatives or enterprises. Applications pass through district, then provincial, then national plant protection, and finally to GACC, which reviews them quarterly. In Sep 2022, 51 of 126 planting areas and 25 of 41 packing houses were approved. Around May 2025, about half of 1,604 proposed areas and 314 packing houses were approved. The article describes codes being leased and traded — [Tuổi Trẻ, 31 Jul 2026](https://tuoitre.vn/quy-trinh-cap-ma-vung-trong-giay-kiem-nghiem-sau-rieng-xuat-khau-sang-trung-quoc-the-nao-100260730182952725.htm)
- On 21 May 2025, GACC approved **829 planting codes and 131 packing houses**, bringing the totals to **1,396 durian planting codes and 188 packing houses** — [PPD (search summary)](https://www.ppd.gov.vn/tin-moi-nhat/trung-quoc-phe-duyet-them-829-ma-so-vung-trong-va-131-ma-so-co-so-dong-goi-sau-rieng-cua-viet-nam.html); [Xây dựng chính sách](https://xaydungchinhsach.chinhphu.vn/them-960-ma-so-vung-trong-co-so-dong-goi-sau-rieng-duoc-xuat-khau-sang-trung-quoc-119250522111816146.htm)
- **Violations from 2025 to May 2026:** China warned **403 planting codes and 240 packing houses**, and **167 planting codes and 99 packing houses** were suspended or revoked. Nationwide there are **9,546 planting-area codes and 1,525 packing-house codes**, of which China has approved **4,323 and 1,332** (all fruits). **Decree 38/2026/NĐ-CP** sets one legal framework for managing these codes, covering suspension, restoration and revocation — [VTV, 15 May 2026](https://vtv.vn/tu-cap-ma-so-den-giu-uy-tin-nong-san-viet-100260515104347387.htm)
- In Vĩnh Long (2025), durian covers 8,140 ha, of which 4,122 ha are commercial. There are 72 export codes (58 active, 14 suspended or revoked) covering 1,611 ha, 14 packing houses, 265.55 ha with VietGAP, and 12 cooperatives plus 44 groups. Farmers complain of price squeezing ("ép giá"), unfair contract terms and delayed harvests. An MAE directive of **6 May 2026** requires traceability systems and stricter code management — [Báo Chính phủ, 26 May 2026](https://baochinhphu.vn/bai-2-go-nut-that-cadimi-tai-thiet-chuoi-sau-rieng-de-giu-thi-truong-xuat-khau-102260524220023812.htm)

**Traceability systems now mandatory or being rolled out**
- **Decision 5272/QĐ-BNNMT** (13 Dec 2025) set up the pilot of the **"Hệ thống truy xuất nguồn gốc nông sản Việt Nam"** (Vietnam agricultural-product traceability system), launched **26 Dec 2025** and run by MAE. It standardises farm logs, input records and pest-control records, and has three parts: a query platform, a **"data-provider solution system"**, and government monitoring tools. Durian is the first product. Five enterprises took part in the pilot from 1 Jan to 30 Jun 2026, and other commodities are planned by end-2026 — [MST](https://mst.gov.vn/ra-mat-he-thong-truy-xuat-nguon-goc-nong-san-viet-nam-197251226211258661.htm)
- The system has been live **nationwide since 1 Jul 2026**, with **a QR code for every lot**. Required data covers codes, farm diaries, packing-house details, harvest and export data, and pesticide-residue records. Farmers are expected to move from paper to electronic logs updated daily. Exporters are legally liable for each shipment. Reported problems include weak monitoring after codes are issued, incomplete diaries, data silos, and code "borrowing" and falsification — [Công Thương, 14 May 2026](https://congthuong.vn/truy-xuat-nguon-goc-tam-ve-giu-thi-truong-ty-usd-cho-sau-rieng-viet-456319.html)
- The **Ministry of Public Security** issues authentication stamps (QR/NFC/RFID) for the durian traceability pilot, which involved at least 5 enterprises from 1 Jan to 30 Jun 2026 — [Thương hiệu & Công luận, 19 Dec 2025](https://thuonghieucongluan.com.vn/thi-diem-truy-xuat-nguon-goc-sau-rieng-bang-tem-do-bo-cong-an-phat-hanh-a296065.html)
- A vendor states that businesses can connect to the national system directly or through certified third-party providers. This is a vendor claim with no legal citation — [An Hà (vendor)](https://temchonghanggia.com/huong-dan-truy-xuat-nguon-goc-sau-rieng-theo-bo-nong-nghiep-va-moi-truong-update-2026/)
- **Law 78/2025/QH15** amending the Law on Product and Goods Quality took effect **1 Jan 2026**. ### Inferences
- The binding constraint is **trustworthy evidence at lot level**, from orchard to packing house to lab to container. - After the July 2026 arrests, any tool that issues or "sells" codes or certificates would be toxic. A tool that makes evidence **auditable and hard to forge** (signed records, photos with timestamps and GPS, lab PDFs linked to lots) fits the political mood.

## Q3. Fisheries and aquaculture: IUU yellow card, VMS/eCDT, US MMPA, EU CATCH; shrimp and pangasius pains

### Takeaway
The EC's **5th inspection (9–19 Mar 2026)** acknowledged progress but **kept the yellow card**, in place since 2017. The remaining failures are on-the-ground enforcement: **VMS disconnections** (2,098 vessels offline for more than 6 hours), **eCDT updated at only about 70%** with about 30% of catch monitored, and slow handling of violation cases. The government aims to lift the card by end-2026. In parallel, the **EU CATCH** digital catch-certificate system became mandatory on **10 Jan 2026**, and the **US MMPA ban on 12 Vietnamese wild fisheries** took effect on **1 Jan 2026**. In aquaculture (shrimp US$4.65 bn, pangasius US$2.19 bn in 2025), pond **success rates of about 40%** remain the chronic pain. US traceability (FSMA 204) has been pushed back to 2028.

### Cited Findings
- Seafood exports were **US$11.34 bn in 2025 (+13%)**: shrimp **US$4.65 bn (+20%)**, pangasius **US$2.19 bn (+8%)**, other marine fish US$2.16 bn. Tuna declined because of raw-material shortages and IUU rules. VASEP's 2026 risks are US tariffs, AD/CVD on shrimp, MMPA bans, the IUU yellow card and diversion competition — [Doanh nhân/Pháp luật, 5 Jan 2026](https://doanhnhan.baophapluat.vn/vasep-xuat-khau-thuy-san-vuot-11-ty-usd-nam-2025-du-bao-con-gap-kho-nam-2026-90444.html). Exports to the US were about US$1.9 bn (+3%), to the EU above US$1.2 bn, and to CPTPP markets above US$3 bn (VASEP data in the same source set; [search summary](https://doanhnhan.baophapluat.vn/vasep-xuat-khau-thuy-san-vuot-11-ty-usd-nam-2025-du-bao-con-gap-kho-nam-2026-90444.html))
- **5th EC inspection, 9–19 Mar 2026.** The EC noted progress on vessels violating foreign waters but cited improper marking and registration, weak port control, weak traceability of imported raw material, and slow case processing. Vietnam has **80,350 registered vessels** of 6 m and over, of which 76,700+ are licensed. **101 vessels** were detained abroad between 2024 and 15 Apr 2026. eCDT is approved at **86 ports** — [Báo Pháp luật, 2 May 2026](https://baophapluat.vn/tang-toc-go-the-vang-iuu-viet-nam-da-va-dang-buoc-vao-chang-quyet-dinh-trong-nam-2026.html)
- **Status on 28 Jul 2026:** the yellow card is still in place after 8 years, and Deputy PM Hồ Quốc Dũng aims to remove it by end-2026. **2,098 vessels** had VMS disconnections longer than 6 hours, **292** were offline for more than 10 days, and **189** crossed authorised boundaries. The **eCDT data-update rate is about 70%** against a 100% requirement, and about 30% of catch is monitored. There have been 8 foreign-waters incidents since Jan 2026 (−86.7%). VMS is installed on **99.6%** of vessels of 15 m and over, and VNFishbase lists 80,332 vessels. Results by **31 Aug 2026** were treated as decisive — [VTV, 28 Jul 2026](https://vtv.vn/pho-thu-tuong-ho-quoc-dung-don-toan-bo-cong-suc-de-go-the-vang-iuu-trong-nam-2026-100260728130332513.htm); see also [Công điện 18/CĐ-TTg 2026 (search result)](https://luatvietnam.vn/xuat-nhap-khau/cong-dien-18-cd-ttg-2026-chuan-bi-lam-viec-voi-doan-thanh-tra-eu-lan-5-go-the-vang-thuy-san-426745-d6.html)
- **EU CATCH IT system**, under Art. 4 of revised Fisheries Control Regulation (EU) 2023/2842: use is **mandatory from 10 Jan 2026** for EU operators and authorities handling imports of fishery products — [European Commission DG MARE](https://oceans-and-fisheries.ec.europa.eu/fisheries/rules/illegal-fishing_en)
- **US MMPA:** NOAA found that 240 foreign fisheries, **12 of them Vietnamese**, failed comparability. The import ban took effect **1 Jan 2026** and covers tuna, swordfish, grouper, mackerel, mullet, crab and squid. Shipments under flagged HTS codes need a **Certificate of Admissibility**. On 31 Oct 2025 the CIT stayed the ban for swimming-crab fisheries pending reconsideration — [NOAA (search summary)](https://www.fisheries.noaa.gov/foreign/marine-mammal-protection/seafood-import-prohibitions-under-marine-mammal-protection-act). A logistics firm estimates about **US$512 m a year** of exports are affected, or 28–30% of Vietnam's US seafood exports. ### Inferences
- Seafood **compliance documentation** (IUU catch certificates via EU CATCH, MMPA Certificates of Admissibility, eCDT reconciliation) is a real 2026 pain point. The data, however, sits in **government systems** (VNFishbase, VMS, eCDT), which makes it hard for students to access.

## Q4. Mekong Delta "1 million ha high-quality, low-emission rice": MRV needs, carbon-credit potential, climate risks

### Takeaway
The programme has **869,013 ha planned for 2026** (86.9% of the 1 M ha target). Only **58,071 ha are registered for MRV** and just **15,534.6 ha (1.8%) are enrolled in carbon finance**, and the press calls MRV "the critical bottleneck". A national MRV procedure (**Decision 4043/QĐ-TTBVTV, 10 Nov 2025**) is being piloted in An Giang, Cần Thơ and Đồng Tháp from the 2025–26 Winter-Spring crop. MRV costs about **300,000 VND/ha**, and one study estimates that IoT, remote sensing and AI could bring it down to about 100,000 VND/ha. In the one disclosed carbon payout, revenue was about **222,000 VND/ha**, which is less than today's MRV cost per hectare.

### Cited Findings
- 2026 figures: **869,013 ha** planned. **58,071 ha** are registered for MRV, of which 56,845 ha are under the TRVC (World Bank-supported rice value-chain) project. Carbon-finance enrolment is **15,534.6 ha (1.8%)** across **9,956 households**. MRV involves field mapping, data collection in **KoboToolbox** and emissions calculation, and most cooperatives lack staff, equipment, digital skills and funding. A An Grain expanded low-emission sourcing from 610 ha to 3,682 ha with 1,200+ farmers. **Vinarice** (a VINASEED subsidiary) covered **27,000 ha** in the 2025 Summer-Autumn crop, reduced emissions by **3.76 tCO₂e/ha**, earned **AUD 339,784 (~6 bn VND)**, and paid 40% to farmers and cooperatives while reinvesting 60%. Its activity logs are 100% digitised — [Nhân Dân, 13 Jul 2026](https://nhandan.vn/go-nut-that-gia-tang-loi-ich-tu-lua-phat-thai-thap-post975204.html)
- **Decision 4043/QĐ-TTBVTV (10 Nov 2025)**, issued by the Department of Crop Production and Plant Protection, sets out low-emission rice and MRV procedures in **6 stages**: preparation, registration, baseline, measurement, reporting and verification. It is piloted in **An Giang, Cần Thơ and Đồng Tháp** from the **2025–26 Winter-Spring** crop. Cooperatives and extension staff collect the data. Claimed reductions are 29–45% from water management (AWD) and more than 80% potential from straw management — [VISTA, 24 Dec 2025](https://vista.gov.vn/vi/news/khoa-hoc-ky-thuat-va-cong-nghe/ap-dung-quy-trinh-mrv-trong-canh-tac-lua-buoc-chuyen-chien-luoc-huong-toi-nong-nghiep-phat-thai-thap-va-thi-truong-carbon-12558.html)
- An **MRV readiness index** (1–5) scored Cần Thơ 4.0, Sóc Trăng 3.6 (tides disrupt AWD), Đồng Tháp 3.4 (manual data), Kiên Giang 3.0 and Trà Vinh 2.8 (limited fresh water). Readiness correlates with achieved reductions (r = 0.95), ranging from 12.0 tCO₂e/ha in Cần Thơ to 3.7 in Trà Vinh. **MRV is about 30% of additional project costs**, and **verification costs about 300,000 VND/ha**. IoT, remote sensing and AI are proposed to bring this to **about 100,000 VND/ha**. Methodology: **Verra VM0051**. Regulations cited: **Decree 29/2026/NĐ-CP** (domestic carbon exchange), Decrees 330/2025 and 323/2025 — [Tạp chí Kinh tế Tài chính, 26 Jun 2026](https://nghiencuu.tapchikinhtetaichinh.vn/tac-dong-chinh-sach-vung-trong-lo-trinh-giam-phat-thai-carbon-nganh-lua-gao-va-muc-do-san-sang-cua-he-thong-mrv-tai-dong-bang-song-cuu-long-160046.html)
- **Cần Thơ** is piloting MRV on **8,000 ha across 30 cooperatives**, starting from 5.5 ha at Thuận Lợi cooperative. The model uses **water-level and fertiliser sensors plus software**. Reported results: +0.89 t/ha yield, +5.5 m VND/ha profit (+24.2%) and −60 kg/ha seed. Farmers' record-keeping is weak — [VietnamPlus, 5 Apr 2026](https://www.vietnamplus.vn/can-tho-canh-cua-dua-lua-gao-viet-vao-thi-truong-tin-chi-carbon-post1102973.vnp)
- **Background (2024):** the World Bank TCAF could pay about US$33–40 m (826–992 bn VND) under an ERPA, with about US$2 m of MRV technical support — [CAND, Sep 2024](https://cand.vn/Kinh-te/nong-dan-dong-bang-song-cuu-long-se-nhan-gan-1-000-ty-dong-chi-tra-tien-tin-chi-carbon-lua-i745108). Emission-reduction reports were bought at US$20/t, and formal credit trading was expected around 2028 — [Tiền Phong, Sep 2024](https://tienphong.vn/de-an-1-trieu-ha-lua-chat-luong-cao-phat-thai-thap-xem-xet-thi-diem-chi-tra-tin-chi-carbon-post1677657.tpo). A figure of "US$10/credit" for World Bank purchases was also reported — [VietnamNet, May 2024](https://vietnamnet.vn/trong-lua-de-ban-duoc-10-usd-tin-chi-carbon-phai-theo-quy-trinh-nghiem-ngat-2277094.html)
- Press coverage stresses digitised household data as the foundation for MRV but names no specific tools or standards — [BNews, 17 Jul 2026](https://bnews.vn/tu-ruong-lua-den-thi-truong-cac-bon/428777.html)

### Inferences
- **The unit economics drive this opportunity.** Vinarice earned about 6 bn VND over 27,000 ha, which is **about 222,000 VND/ha**. MRV currently costs **about 300,000 VND/ha**. Carbon credits only pay off for farmers if MRV becomes much cheaper, and an AI and remote-sensing MRV agent attacks exactly that cost line.
- The most likely payers are **aggregators and exporters** (A An, Vinarice, Lộc Trời-type firms), **project developers**, and **donor or state programmes**. Individual farmers are unlikely to pay.
- Salinity and tides lower MRV readiness in coastal provinces (Sóc Trăng, Trà Vinh), so the MRV agent needs to be climate-aware.

### Gaps
- The **status of the TCAF ERPA in 2026** (whether it was signed and how much was paid) and the **buyer** of Vinarice's credits were not found.
- I could not find out whether Decision 4043 accepts **remote sensing as evidence for AWD verification**.
- No 2025–26 **salinity** data (the area affected in the 2025–26 dry season) was obtained.
- The founding decision of the programme (reported as Decision 1490/QĐ-TTg, Nov 2023) was not re-verified in this research.

## Q5. Existing Vietnamese and international agritech players: what they do and where the gaps are

### Takeaway
Vietnam already has plenty of **farm-diary, QR/blockchain traceability and photo-based disease-diagnosis apps** (iCheck, TraceVerified, VNPT Check, Viettel vCheck, 2Nông, Tép Bạc/Farmext). International EUDR platforms (Koltiva, TraceX, Farmforce, Satelligence, Enveritas, Kanop) serve large coffee buyers. I found **no product** that acts as an **agent**: one that reads market rules, plans pre-harvest compliance, books labs, assembles lot-level dossiers, pushes data to the national system, and keeps working without being prompted at each step. ### Inferences
- **Saturated in Vietnam:** photo-based disease diagnosis, farming Q&A chatbots, QR/blockchain stamps, generic e-farm diaries, IoT water sensors on their own, and satellite deforestation checks on their own.

Instead of competing with iCheck or the national system, it would read and write to them, and **plan and act** to reach compliance outcomes: a harvestable, test-passing, dossier-ready lot. It could also become a certified "data provider" to the national system, if the rules allow it (see the Q2 gap).

### Gaps

### Inferences
**Target users and payer**
- Users: farmers (voice and Zalo logging), **cooperative and planting-code managers**, **packing-house QA staff**, and **exporter compliance staff**.
- Payers: **packing houses and exporters** first, since they carry per-shipment legal liability and container risk. Cooperatives pay second, possibly with exporter or provincial co-funding. Farmers use it for free.

**Agentic workflow** (multi-agent, with memory per plot and per lot, and human approval at key points)
1. **Diary agent:** takes Vietnamese voice notes and photos of pesticide labels over Zalo and turns them into structured logs (input, active ingredient, dose, date, block). It asks follow-up questions when a field is missing and keeps per-plot history in memory.
2. **Compliance-rules agent:** checks each spray against the destination market's MRL and banned-substance lists and against the pre-harvest interval, then computes the **earliest safe harvest date per block**. It also builds a **cadmium and Auramine O risk profile** from soil tests, fertiliser sources and the trader handling step.
3. **Sampling and lab agent:** chooses risk-based pre-tests, **books a slot at one of the 37 GACC-accepted labs** by email, Zalo or a portal, balances load across labs, tracks results, and re-plans harvest or lot segregation when a result fails.
4. **Lot and dossier agent:** keeps lots separate by code so fruit is not mixed, and assembles the dossier: code, packing-house code, diary extract, lab certificate, phytosanitary request data. It pushes the data to the **national traceability system** (API to be confirmed) and generates a **QR / digital-product-passport page per lot**.
5. **Regulatory-watch agent:** monitors PPD/MAE/GACC notices, lab-list updates, code warnings and suspensions, and new decrees. It proposes rule updates, which an agronomist approves.
6. **Human-in-the-loop:** the cooperative manager approves dossiers, the exporter signs off before submission, and every action is logged so it can be audited after the July 2026 case.
- Tools and APIs: Zalo OA / Mini App, Vietnamese speech-to-text and OCR, an LLM with function calling, an MRL/PHI rules database, email and calendar, PDF and QR generation, GIS polygons, a weather API, and the national system connector.

**MVP (~7 weeks)**
- Zalo bot with voice and photo diary, a rules database for about 30 common durian pesticides, a harvest-readiness planner, lab booking (real by email or simulated), a lot dossier PDF with a public QR page, a regulatory-watch scraper for PPD news, a manager dashboard, and an approval queue.
- Pilot with 1 cooperative or planting code (10–20 farmers) and 1 packing house.
- KPIs: diary completeness %, time to produce a dossier, number of pre-harvest risk flags, lab turnaround.

**Pilot access near HCMC**
- Đồng Nai (the Dầu Giây container hub, flagged in [Dân Trí](https://dantri.com.vn/kinh-doanh/nut-that-kiem-dinh-sau-rieng-co-tien-van-kho-mo-phong-xet-nghiem-20260518081939827.htm)).

- Labs are concentrated in HCMC (15).
- Possible partners: VINAFRUIT and the Đắk Lắk Durian Association.

**Revenue model and pricing** (all prices are assumptions)
- About **4 m VND per planting code per year** (≈US$154; a code is a group of 10 ha or more, compared with Farmext at US$82 per farm).
- About **36 m VND per packing house or exporter per year**.

**Market size** (counts from [VTV](https://vtv.vn/tu-cap-ma-so-den-giu-uy-tin-nong-san-viet-100260515104347387.htm); prices are assumptions)

### Inferences
**Ranked shortlist for the report writer**
1. **(a) Agentic Export-Compliance Passport, durian first (35/40).** It has the most specific why-now hooks with exact dates:
   - Decision 5272/QĐ-BNNMT, 13 Dec 2025.
   - National system live 1 Jul 2026.
   - Decree 38/2026/NĐ-CP.
   - Resolution 36/2026/NQ-CP.
   - MAE directive of 6 May 2026.
   - Law 78/2025/QH15, effective 1 Jan 2026.
   - Arrests on 28 Jul 2026.

   It also has the best poster-booth demo (scanning a durian QR passport) and pilot access through Đồng Nai, Vĩnh Long, Đồng Tháp and the TDTU Bảo Lộc campus. The pitch should make clear that it **complements the national system** (integration, or a certified data-provider role) and should not present itself as another QR vendor.
2. **(c) Rice MRV agent (28/40).** The best climate-impact story. The pitch would centre on cutting MRV cost from about 300k to about 100k VND/ha. Finding a payer has to be addressed in the business plan, for example with exporters like A An or Vinarice as anchor customers.
3. **(b) EUDR agent (27/40).** Best positioned as an **expansion module of (a)**, or a stand-alone product for SME, cooperative and timber segments. Its strongest hooks are the 30 Dec 2026 start date (during Round 2) and the 30 Jun 2027 start for micro and small operators.
4. **(d) Shrimp co-pilot (25/40).** Only worth pursuing as a partner integration (for example, an agent layer on Farmext) with Cà Mau campus access.
5. **(e) Seafood compliance (23/40).** Not recommended for a student team because of government data access and sensitivity.

**Positioning against ideas from other domains**
- Strengths of this domain:
  - Hard, dated regulatory triggers in 2026–27.
  - Large export values (US$3.85 bn durian; US$3.63 bn coffee to the EU; US$4.65 bn shrimp).
  - TDTU campuses in Bảo Lộc, Cà Mau and Nha Trang give unusual field access.
  - Agentic AI fits naturally, because compliance is a multi-step, tool-using workflow.
- Weaknesses of this domain:
  - Low willingness to pay among farmers.
  - Seasonality.
  - Risk of being displaced by state platforms.
  - Political sensitivity after the 2026 arrests.
  - Primary willingness-to-pay validation (Round 1 survey) is required.

**Saturated ideas to avoid** (or include only as features)
- AI photo diagnosis of crop or shrimp disease.
- General farming Q&A chatbots or "virtual agronomists".
- QR, blockchain or anti-counterfeit traceability stamps.
- Stand-alone e-farm diaries.
- Stand-alone IoT water sensors.
- Satellite deforestation checks on their own for Vietnamese coffee.
- Agri price and weather apps, and generic agri marketplaces.

### Gaps
- No primary willingness-to-pay data exists for any candidate. Round 1 should include interviews with 10–20 packing houses or exporters for (a), 3–5 exporters for (b), and 3–5 cooperatives or aggregators for (c).
- Whether third parties can access APIs for (i) MAE's national traceability system and (ii) the EU EUDR Information System is unverified, and it decides how much of the "real action" claim is achievable by January 2027.
- Weather, salinity and climate early warning was considered as a social-impact add-on. It was not scored, because no current 2025–26 salinity data was collected and willingness to pay is likely low.
