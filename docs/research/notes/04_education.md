## 1. Policy changes 2024–2026 and their effects (the "why now")

### Takeaway
2025–2026 brought an unusually large set of education policy changes:
- tighter extra-class rules (Circular 29/2024, amended by Circular 19/2026);
- a Politburo "breakthrough" resolution (71-NQ/TW);
- an English-as-second-language programme (Decision 2371/QĐ-TTg);
- a pilot K-12 AI curriculum (Decision 3439/QĐ-BGDĐT);
- a stable exam format, with computer-based testing from June 2027;
- new university admission rules (Circular 06/2026);
- a nationwide rollout of digital school records;
- a shortage of 104,516 teachers.

Together these create demand for three kinds of tools: ones that save teachers time, ones that deliver free in-school remediation, and ones that handle complicated rules on behalf of students.

### Cited Findings
**Extra classes (dạy thêm, học thêm)**
- Circular 29/2024/TT-BGDĐT was issued 30/12/2024 and took effect 14/02/2025 — [LuatVietnam](https://luatvietnam.vn/tin-van-ban-moi/da-co-thong-tu-29-2024-tt-bgddt-quy-dinh-ve-day-them-hoc-them-tu-14-02-2025-186-100582-article.html); [Thư viện Pháp luật](https://thuvienphapluat.vn/van-ban/Giao-duc/Thong-tu-29-2024-TT-BGDDT-quy-dinh-day-them-hoc-them-622469.aspx). The 30/12/2024 issue date comes from the Hanoi DoET summary of the amendment: [Sở GD&ĐT Hà Nội](https://hanoi.edu.vn/tin-tuc-su-kien/sua-doi-bo-sung-mot-so-dieu-cua-thong-tu-quy-dinh-ve-day-them-hoc-them/ctmb/525/16891).
- Core rules — [Giáo dục Việt Nam – 10 điểm mới](https://giaoduc.net.vn/tong-hop-10-diem-moi-cua-thong-tu-29-quy-dinh-ve-day-them-hoc-them-post248291.gd); [LuatVietnam](https://luatvietnam.vn/tin-van-ban-moi/da-co-thong-tu-29-2024-tt-bgddt-quy-dinh-ve-day-them-hoc-them-tu-14-02-2025-186-100582-article.html):
  - In-school extra classes **must be free** and are open only to three groups: students whose result in the subject was "failing", gifted students selected by the school, and final-year students who volunteer for entrance or graduation exam review.
  - Maximum **2 sessions per subject per week**.
  - Teachers **may not tutor their own students for a fee** outside school.
  - No extra classes for primary pupils, except arts, sports and life skills.
  - Out-of-school tutoring must be registered as a business.
  - Teachers who tutor outside school must report the subject, place and time to the principal.
- **Circular 19/2026/TT-BGDĐT** (issued 31/03/2026, effective 15/05/2026) amends Circular 29 — [Sở GD&ĐT Hà Nội](https://hanoi.edu.vn/tin-tuc-su-kien/sua-doi-bo-sung-mot-so-dieu-cua-thong-tu-quy-dinh-ve-day-them-hoc-them/ctmb/525/16891):
  - It removes from the regulated scope activities in culture, arts, sports, foreign languages, STEM/STEAM, digital literacy, **AI education, career guidance** and life skills.
  - Principals may propose extra sessions for particular students to the Department Director.
  - The 2-sessions-per-week cap stays the norm.
  - Hotlines are set up at school, commune and Department level.
  - Tutoring centres must publish their curriculum, duration, teacher list and fees.
- The amendment summary contains **no statistics** on what Circular 29 changed in practice (centres closed, students affected) — [Sở GD&ĐT Hà Nội](https://hanoi.edu.vn/tin-tuc-su-kien/sua-doi-bo-sung-mot-so-dieu-cua-thong-tu-quy-dinh-ve-day-them-hoc-them/ctmb/525/16891).

**Politburo Resolution 71-NQ/TW**
- Signed 22/08/2025 by General Secretary Tô Lâm, on breakthrough development of education and training — [Xây dựng chính sách (full text)](https://xaydungchinhsach.chinhphu.vn/toan-van-nghi-quyet-so-71-nq-tw-cua-bo-chinh-tri-ve-dot-pha-phat-trien-giao-duc-va-dao-tao-119250828110759964.htm).
- 2030 targets (same source):
  - ≥80% of secondary schools meet national standards;
  - ≥85% upper-secondary completion;
  - 50% of the relevant age group in post-secondary education;
  - 24% of the workforce with an associate degree or higher;
  - ≥35% of learners in STEM;
  - 8 universities in Asia's top 200.
- Budget: education gets **≥20% of total state spending**, including ≥5% for capital investment and ≥3% for higher education (same source).
- Teacher allowances: at least 70% (for preschool and primary teachers, per the fetch summary), at least 30% for support staff, and 100% in remote or minority areas (same source). Check in the full text exactly which teachers the 70% covers.
- The resolution also calls for a data strategy, a national education platform, digital and AI-enhanced teaching, gradually making English a second language in schools, and free textbooks for all students by 2030. This is from a fetch summary; check the exact wording in the full text.

**English as a second language**

**AI curriculum and digital competency**
- **Decision 3439/QĐ-BGDĐT** (15/12/2025) issues the pilot AI-education content framework for K-12 — [MOET](https://moet.gov.vn/tin-tuc/ban-hanh-khung-noi-dung-thi-diem-giao-duc-tri-tue-nhan-tao-ai-cho-hoc-sinh-pho-thong2.html).
  - It has 4 strands: human-centred thinking, AI ethics, AI techniques and applications, and AI system design.
  - It has 2 stages: basic (primary and lower secondary) and career-oriented (upper secondary).
- It builds on **Circular 02/2025/TT-BGDĐT** (24/01/2025), the digital competency framework for learners — same source.
- The fetched pages give no pilot start date or number of periods — [VOV2](https://vov2.vov.vn/giao-duc-dao-tao/bo-gd-dt-ban-hanh-khung-noi-dung-thi-diem-day-tri-tue-nhan-tao-ai-tu-tieu-hoc).

**National high-school exam**
- Format since 2025 — [FPT University explainer](https://daihoc.fpt.edu.vn/tuyen-sinh/thi-tot-nghiep-thpt-may-mon/):
  - 4 subjects: Math and Literature are compulsory, plus 2 electives.
  - **Literature is the only essay subject**: 120 minutes, Reading and Writing.
  - Other subjects use three question types: 4-option multiple choice, true/false clusters of 4 statements, and short answer.
- Candidate numbers: 2025 had **1,165,289** registered candidates — [Giáo dục Việt Nam](https://giaoduc.net.vn/ky-thi-tot-nghiep-thpt-2026-co-5-diem-moi-chuan-bi-lo-trinh-thi-tren-may-tinh-tu-nam-2027-post254747.gd). 2026 had "over 1.2 million" registered — [Đại biểu Nhân dân](https://daibieunhandan.vn/bo-gd-dt-de-thi-tot-nghiep-thpt-2026-se-phan-hoa-de-dap-ung-xet-tuyen-dai-hoc-10418753.html).
- The 2026 exam ran on 11–12/06/2026 with a 40-30-30 split across recognition, comprehension and application — same two sources.
- Other 2026 changes: grade appeals cut from 10 to 5 days, fewer documents, direct data transmission — [Giáo dục Việt Nam](https://giaoduc.net.vn/ky-thi-tot-nghiep-thpt-2026-co-5-diem-moi-chuan-bi-lo-trinh-thi-tren-may-tinh-tu-nam-2027-post254747.gd); [Tuổi Trẻ](https://tuoitre.vn/thi-tot-nghiep-thpt-nam-2026-co-mot-so-dieu-chinh-2027-thi-tren-may-tinh-20250926143722753.htm).
- Literature word limits since 2025: 200-word paragraph and 600-word essay — [Giáo dục Việt Nam, 06/07/2025](https://giaoduc.net.vn/gioi-han-so-chu-trong-bai-thi-ngu-van-thi-sinh-viet-trong-tam-bot-my-tu-sao-rong-post252567.gd). Effects reported by graders:
  - grading took 6 days instead of 8;
  - 40 candidates produced about 45 answer sheets instead of 75–80.
- **Computer-based testing (CBT) roadmap** — [Tuổi Trẻ](https://tuoitre.vn/thi-tot-nghiep-thpt-nam-2026-co-mot-so-dieu-chinh-2027-thi-tren-may-tinh-20250926143722753.htm); [Giáo dục Việt Nam](https://giaoduc.net.vn/ky-thi-tot-nghiep-thpt-2026-co-5-diem-moi-chuan-bi-lo-trinh-thi-tren-may-tinh-tu-nam-2027-post254747.gd):
  - Apr–May 2026: trial with about 100,000 candidates;
  - Jul 2026: proposal to the Government;
  - Oct–Dec 2026: procedures issued and pilot sites chosen;
  - Jun 2027: CBT at qualified sites, paper elsewhere.
- CBT trials so far — [VietnamPlus](https://www.vietnamplus.vn/nam-2027-se-thi-tot-nghiep-thpt-song-song-hinh-thuc-tren-giay-va-truc-tuyen-post1126830.vnp):
  - late 2024: more than 12,000 students;
  - early 2026: 36,000 test-takers and about 143,000 test attempts in 15 provinces;
  - CBT is described for **multiple-choice subjects**, using the same content as the paper version.
- MOET is building a standardised question bank — [VTV](https://vtv.vn/thi-tot-nghiep-thpt-tren-may-tinh-tu-2027-buoc-chuyen-lon-trong-chuyen-doi-so-giao-duc-100260503001917565.htm).

**University admissions (Circular 06/2026/TT-BGDĐT)** — [Xây dựng chính sách](https://xaydungchinhsach.chinhphu.vn/nhung-diem-moi-trong-quy-che-tuyen-sinh-dai-hoc-2026-119260215183555963.htm); [Tuổi Trẻ, 28/02/2026](https://tuoitre.vn/tuyen-sinh-dai-hoc-2026-nhung-thay-doi-thi-sinh-can-luu-y-20260228075633729.htm)
- Each candidate may list up to **15 choices (nguyện vọng)**; teacher-training programmes may only be choices 1–5.
- Each university may use at most **5 admission methods**.
- Transcript-based admission must use all 6 semesters and at least 3 subjects, with Math or Literature making up at least 1/3 of the weight.
- Exam-based admission needs at least **15/30** points.
- Total bonus points are capped at **3/30**.
- The exam moved about 2 weeks earlier. Administrators warn the new constraints add "thêm áp lực cho thí sinh" (extra pressure on candidates) — [Tuổi Trẻ](https://tuoitre.vn/tuyen-sinh-dai-hoc-2026-nhung-thay-doi-thi-sinh-can-luu-y-20260228075633729.htm).

**Digital school records (học bạ số)**
- MOET's 2025–2026 goal is a nationwide rollout of digital school records at all levels, plus digital diplomas from upper secondary through postgraduate — [VietnamPlus](https://www.vietnamplus.vn/nganh-giao-duc-day-manh-chuyen-doi-so-ung-dung-tri-tue-nhan-tao-nam-hoc-moi-post1058660.vnp).
- Pilot status — [Thiếu niên](https://thieunien.vn/pha-t-ha-nh-4-trie-u-ho-so-ho-c-ba-so-tbd67966.html):
  - 7 Departments of Education piloted the connection to the national database: Hà Nội, **TP.HCM**, Hải Phòng, Ninh Bình, Vĩnh Long, Gia Lai and Hưng Yên.
  - By **08/07/2026, more than 4 million records** had been issued and synced.
  - School-software vendors connected to the national database through APIs.

**Teacher shortage**
- As of June 2026 the country was **104,516 teachers** short of staffing norms — [Giáo dục Việt Nam, 25/07/2026](https://giaoduc.net.vn/tinh-den-thang-62026-ca-nuoc-con-thieu-104516-giao-vien-bien-che-so-voi-dinh-muc-post261626.gd):
  - preschool 14,757; primary 30,949; lower secondary 42,357; upper secondary 16,453;
  - 14,994 teachers were recruited in 2025–2026;
  - **HCMC filled 4,595 of its 7,114 positions**.
- There are about **1.3 million teachers** nationwide, and more than 40,000 approved posts were still unfilled at 31/05/2026. The Minister blames planning lags, slow recruitment and subject imbalances — [Afamily](https://afamily.vn/bo-truong-bo-gddt-neu-nguyen-nhan-thieu-104500-giao-vien-236260920144943244.chn).

**2025–2026 school-year priorities** — [Báo Chính phủ](https://baochinhphu.vn/giao-duc-pho-thong-buoc-nam-hoc-2025-2026-doi-moi-sau-rong-102250805172909408.htm)
- Two teaching sessions per day in primary schools.
- New teaching and assessment methods.
- Digital transformation, including continuing the digital school records.
- AI applied to **career guidance and personalised learning**.
- English as a second language.

**University academic rules**
- **Circular 56/2026/TT-BGDĐT**, as reported in September 2026, sets when a student gets an academic warning — [Báo Gia Lai, 16/09/2026](https://baogialai.com.vn/bi-canh-bao-hoc-tap-bao-nhieu-lan-sinh-vien-co-the-bi-buoc-thoi-hoc-post599166.html):
  - failed credits exceed 50% of the semester's registered load;
  - more than 24 failed credits accumulated;
  - semester GPA below 0.8 (first semester) or below 1.0 (later semesters);
  - cumulative GPA below 1.2, 1.4, 1.6 or 1.8 in years 1, 2, 3 and 4+.
- A student is dismissed (buộc thôi học) when warnings exceed the university's limit or the maximum study period is exceeded (same source).

### Inferences
- **Remediation now runs through schools.** Circular 29 makes the school the legal channel for helping weak students, and that help must be free. Schools therefore need cheap ways to see who needs help and to run it, and teacher time is the bottleneck (104.5k shortage). An agent that turns grading data into remediation groups and materials fits the policy exactly.
- **Less friction for some private providers.** Circular 19/2026 takes AI education, career guidance, STEM and life skills out of the "extra class" scope. Private products in those categories face less regulation than subject tutoring.
- **Literature likely stays handwritten through 2027.** CBT from 2027 covers multiple-choice subjects, and no source says Literature moves to computers. Most in-class tests are also on paper. This is unverified.
- **Integration goes through vendors.** Digital school records already connect vendors to the national database by API. A startup would need to partner with school-management software vendors rather than connect directly.
- **Admissions are now complex.** 15 choices, up to 5 methods per university, transcript rules and bonus caps make planning hard for 1.2 million candidates a year. That complexity is the reason a rules-aware agent could help.

### Gaps
- No numbers found on Circular 29's effects (tutoring demand, edtech uptake, teacher income). The search budget ran out.
- Resolution 71's exact wording on AI was not checked line by line.
- The pilot schedule and periods for Decision 3439 were not found.
- No source confirms whether Literature stays on paper in the 2027 CBT.
- Circular 56/2026 was seen only through a secondary article. The full text was not verified.
- The data-protection rules (Decree 13/2023/NĐ-CP and the 2025 Personal Data Protection Law, reportedly effective 01/01/2026) were **not verified** here. Check them before citing in the risk section.

## 2. Teacher workload evidence (grading, exam creation, paperwork)

### Takeaway
I found no national survey of teacher hours or paperwork. Practitioner reporting is consistent, though:
- a Literature teacher with 4–5 classes grades about 200 essays per periodic test round, each 4–8 handwritten pages (roughly 1,000 pages);
- there are 6 tests per class per semester;
- since 2009, grading time no longer counts toward teaching load;
- the 2025 rubric change made scoring more subjective;
- word limits cut exam-grading time by about 25%.

### Cited Findings
- Literature workload — [Giáo dục Việt Nam, 02/12/2024](https://giaoduc.net.vn/giao-vien-mon-nao-dang-phai-soan-cham-bai-nhieu-nhat-o-bac-hoc-pho-thong-post247470.gd) [background]:
  - 4 regular and 2 periodic tests per class per semester;
  - 4–5 classes per teacher;
  - about 200 essays per periodic test round, 4–8 pages each (90-minute test), "nearly 1,000 pages";
  - about 10 lesson plans per week.
- Grading credit history — [Giáo dục Việt Nam, 11/12/2024](https://giaoduc.net.vn/nganh-gd-tung-co-30-nam-quy-doi-thoi-gian-cham-bai-ngu-van-thanh-tiet-day-post247722.gd) [background]:
  - From 1979 to 2009, Circular 49 counted grading as teaching time: 45 full-length essays = 7 standard periods, with a cap of 90 essays a month.
  - Circular 28/2009 removed this.
  - Standard weekly loads are 23 periods (primary), 19 (lower secondary) and 17 (upper secondary).
- Grade-10 entrance exam grading for Literature — [Giáo dục Việt Nam, 14/06/2024](https://giaoduc.net.vn/noi-vat-va-ap-luc-cua-giao-vien-khi-cham-bai-thi-ngu-van-vao-10-post243374.gd) [background]:
  - graders read "hundreds of pages" a day;
  - 10 scoring components;
  - double grading, with a written report whenever two graders differ by more than 0.75 points;
  - "8 stressful days" of grading.
- 2025 rubric changes for graduation-exam preparation — [Giáo dục Việt Nam, 23/04/2025](https://giaoduc.net.vn/de-xuat-cham-diem-mon-ngu-van-moi-gay-phan-van-cho-giao-vien-post250770.gd):
  - components cut from 5 to 3;
  - new deductions for errors: 5–7 errors cost 0.5 points, 8–10 errors cost 0.75;
  - teachers flagged subjectivity and conflicts with official answer keys.
- Word limits shortened grading from 8 to 6 days — [Giáo dục Việt Nam, 06/07/2025](https://giaoduc.net.vn/gioi-han-so-chu-trong-bai-thi-ngu-van-thi-sinh-viet-trong-tam-bot-my-tu-sao-rong-post252567.gd).
- "1–2 days to grade one class's Literature essays" — snippet-level, attributed to Giáo dục Việt Nam in a search summary and not confirmed in fetched text — [Giáo dục Việt Nam](https://giaoduc.net.vn/giao-vien-mon-nao-dang-phai-soan-cham-bai-nhieu-nhat-o-bac-hoc-pho-thong-post247470.gd).
- A search summary said that after proctoring survey tests, teachers spend about a week grading, returning and revising papers, sometimes unpaid. This is snippet-level, and it is unclear which of these two Giáo dục Việt Nam articles it came from: [grade-12 survey tests](https://giaoduc.net.vn/co-nhat-thiet-phai-to-chuc-khao-sat-hoc-sinh-lop-12-post240811.gd) [background] or [proctoring/grading pressure](https://giaoduc.net.vn/tham-gia-coi-thi-cham-thi-kha-ap-luc-vay-giao-vien-duoc-nhung-gi-post252414.gd). Verify before use.

### Inferences
- Rough scale: 4–5 classes × 6 tests per semester gives about 24–30 grading rounds per semester. If each takes 1–2 days per class (snippet-level), grading takes weeks of evenings each semester. The magnitude is uncertain.
- The real bottleneck is individual comments and tracking each student's recurring errors, not the score itself. AI adds most value through draft feedback and remediation plans that the teacher approves, rather than through autonomous scoring.
- The 2025 rubric deducts for counted errors (spelling, punctuation, word choice). Error counting can be partly automated, which makes scoring more consistent across teachers.

### Gaps
- No national survey (MOET or teachers' union) on weekly hours, paperwork or test-writing time. The search budget ran out.
- No data on how long it takes to write tests in the new formats (true/false clusters, short answer).
- No teacher-attrition statistics for 2025–2026.

## 3. Existing EdTech in Vietnam with AI features, market size, and global benchmarks

### Takeaway
The obvious AI-for-teachers features are already taken in Vietnam. **Azota** offers:
- AI essay grading that accepts legible handwriting, paid per use with Points;
- OCR of exam papers;
- AI question classification;
- free scanning of multiple-choice answer sheets.

OLM, VioEdu and SHub cover the LMS, test-matrix and adaptive-practice layer. B2C AI tutors and English apps (ELSA, Prep, TAK12) are crowded.

Market-size estimates disagree. IMARC puts Vietnam's edtech market at about USD 1.1B in 2025. A local 2026 report says USD 5.1B, apparently using a broader definition.

### Cited Findings
**Market size and investment**
- IMARC: Vietnam edtech was **USD 1.1B in 2025** and is forecast to reach USD 3.2B by 2034 (CAGR 12.31%, 2026–2034). Key players listed: ILA, VUS, Topica, HOCMAI — [IMARC](https://www.imarcgroup.com/vietnam-edtech-market).
- Vietnam EdTech Report 2026 (16th edition, Nguyễn Trí Hiển, updated 04/2026) — [SlideShare](https://www.slideshare.net/slideshow/b-c-tranh-toan-c-nh-th-tr-ng-edtech-elearning-vi-t-nam-2025-2026-update-4-2026/286932352):
  - market size **USD 5.1B**, which conflicts with IMARC, probably because of a broader scope;
  - more than **USD 55M** invested in Vietnamese edtech in 2025;
  - deals: Kyna USD 10M (Jan 2025), Manabie USD 23M (Apr 2025), Galaxy Education USD 10M (Jun 2025), Vuihoc (Sep 2025, undisclosed);
  - top sites by traffic in Feb 2026: Vietjack, Loigiaihay, Hoidap247, Tuyensinh247, OLM;

**K-12 teacher tools**

**Research on how accurate AI grading is**
- "LLMs Do Not Grade Essays Like Humans" — [arXiv 2603.23714](https://arxiv.org/html/2603.23714v1):
  - datasets: ASAP and DREsS_New; models from GPT-3.5 to GPT-5 mini and Llama 4;
  - human–human agreement QWK = 0.72, while LLM–human agreement was **below 0.30**;
  - LLMs compress the score range and over-penalise minor language errors;
  - the authors recommend using LLMs as support tools, with calibration.
- Human-in-the-loop grading of handwritten math — [arXiv 2603.13083](https://arxiv.org/html/2603.13083):
  - setting: VUB, using GPT-5.1;
  - each answer is graded 5 times independently and flagged when the grades disagree; instructors make the final call;
  - human–LLM agreement QWK 0.50–0.91, against 0.70–0.96 between humans;
  - grading time fell by about **23%** (CI 1.1%–40.5%).
- A synthesis of 65 studies (2022–Aug 2025) finds LLM–human agreement is "highly context-dependent" — snippet-level — [ResearchGate](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/397039628_Agreement_Between_Large_Language_Models_and_Human_Raters_in_Essay_Scoring_A_Research_Synthesis).

### Inferences
- **Grading alone won't stand out.** A Vietnamese "AI grades essays" product will be compared directly with Azota, which already has the feature, a big teacher base and per-use pricing. The defensible ground is what happens after grading:
  - per-student error profiles that persist across tests;
  - remediation groups and schedules that follow Circular 29;
  - practice materials and re-assessment;
  - messages to parents;
  - draft comments for digital school records;
  - all with teacher approval.
- **Accuracy limits the design.** The research rules out autonomous final scores for Vietnamese essays. The design has to be human-in-the-loop: draft scores with evidence, uncertainty flags, and calibration on essays the teacher has already graded. Time saved (about 23% in the math study, 80% claimed by CoGrader) is a better pitch metric than "accuracy".
- **Pricing anchors.** Teachers pay little: Azota VIP is about USD 38 a year and MagicSchool Plus about USD 100 a year. School or district licences and pay-per-use credits are the realistic models.

### Gaps
- Not verified for 2025–2026: Hocmai, Vuihoc and Monkey user numbers and AI features, Azota's Point prices, OLM paid plans, ELSA's current figures, and SHub's AI features.
- No Vietnamese benchmark found for university student-success software or its pricing.

## 5. Saturated ideas and how a truly agentic product can differentiate

### Takeaway
Avoid anything that is a Q&A chatbot, generic AI tutor, flashcard or quiz generator, multiple-choice grader, proctoring tool or English-pronunciation app. Incumbents already cover these at scale, often for free. Judges have also heard "Agentic AI" at EDTECH VIETNAM 2026, so the agent has to visibly **do work across systems**, not just answer.

### Inferences
**Saturated ideas to avoid** (likely common among student teams too):
1. General AI tutor or homework chatbot.
2. Flashcard, quiz or exam generators for the new 3-format exam.
3. Scanning and grading multiple-choice answer sheets.
4. Online proctoring and anti-cheating.
5. English pronunciation, speaking or IELTS apps.
6. Lesson-plan and slide generators.
7. University-admissions or career Q&A chatbots.
8. Japanese/Korean vocabulary apps for consumers.
9. University FAQ chatbots that take no actions.

**What makes a product convincingly agentic in this domain:**
- **It closes the loop:** diagnose → plan → act → re-assess → report.
- **It takes real actions through tools and APIs** (Zalo Official Account, Google Calendar/Classroom, LMS/Moodle, e-forms, school-management exports), with each action queued for approval.
- **It remembers each learner** across semesters.
- **It knows the rules:** Circular 29's caps, Circular 56/2026's warning thresholds, ESD language requirements, Circular 06/2026's admission limits.
- **It reports measurable results:** hours saved, warnings avoided, test pass rates.

- No public list of TDTU or Vietnamese student-competition entries was found to prove that AI tutors dominate student teams. The saturation claim rests on market incumbents, not competition data.

## 6. Candidate A — "Trợ giảng AI": agentic grading of handwritten work plus a closed remediation loop (K-12, Literature first)

### Takeaway
This idea has the strongest pain and policy fit in K-12. The main problem is novelty against Azota's AI essay grading. Pitch it as a **remediation agent that also drafts grades**, not an AI grader.

### Cited Findings
- **Pain**:
  - ~200 Literature essays of 4–8 pages per periodic test round, 6 tests per class per semester — [Giáo dục Việt Nam](https://giaoduc.net.vn/giao-vien-mon-nao-dang-phai-soan-cham-bai-nhieu-nhat-o-bac-hoc-pho-thong-post247470.gd) [background];
  - grading no longer counts toward teaching load since 2009 — [Giáo dục Việt Nam](https://giaoduc.net.vn/nganh-gd-tung-co-30-nam-quy-doi-thoi-gian-cham-bai-ngu-van-thanh-tiet-day-post247722.gd) [background];
  - the 2025 rubric made scoring more subjective — [Giáo dục Việt Nam](https://giaoduc.net.vn/de-xuat-cham-diem-mon-ngu-van-moi-gay-phan-van-cho-giao-vien-post250770.gd);
  - shortage of 104,516 teachers, and HCMC filled only 4,595 of 7,114 posts — [Giáo dục Việt Nam](https://giaoduc.net.vn/tinh-den-thang-62026-ca-nuoc-con-thieu-104516-giao-vien-bien-che-so-voi-dinh-muc-post261626.gd).
- **Why now**:
  - Circular 29 (effective 14/02/2025): in-school remediation must be free, only for listed groups, at most 2 sessions per week — [Giáo dục Việt Nam](https://giaoduc.net.vn/tong-hop-10-diem-moi-cua-thong-tu-29-quy-dinh-ve-day-them-hoc-them-post248291.gd);
  - Circular 19/2026 (effective 15/05/2026) lets principals propose extra sessions — [Sở GD&ĐT Hà Nội](https://hanoi.edu.vn/tin-tuc-su-kien/sua-doi-bo-sung-mot-so-dieu-cua-thong-tu-quy-dinh-ve-day-them-hoc-them/ctmb/525/16891);
  - the 2025 exam's word limits (200 and 600 words) make essays shorter and easier to process — [Giáo dục Việt Nam](https://giaoduc.net.vn/gioi-han-so-chu-trong-bai-thi-ngu-van-thi-sinh-viet-trong-tam-bot-my-tu-sao-rong-post252567.gd);
  - MOET's 2025–2026 tasks include AI for personalised learning — [Báo Chính phủ](https://baochinhphu.vn/giao-duc-pho-thong-buoc-nam-hoc-2025-2026-doi-moi-sau-rong-102250805172909408.htm);
  - the digital school-record APIs exist (HCMC is a pilot), with more than 4M records synced by 08/07/2026 — [Thiếu niên](https://thieunien.vn/pha-t-ha-nh-4-trie-u-ho-so-ho-c-ba-so-tbd67966.html).
- **Market anchors**: about 1.3M teachers — [Afamily](https://afamily.vn/bo-truong-bo-gddt-neu-nguyen-nhan-thieu-104500-giao-vien-236260920144943244.chn); 1,165,289 exam candidates in 2025 — [Giáo dục Việt Nam](https://giaoduc.net.vn/ky-thi-tot-nghiep-thpt-2026-co-5-diem-moi-chuan-bi-lo-trinh-thi-tren-may-tinh-tu-nam-2027-post254747.gd); Azota VIP at 999,000 VND/year — [Azota](https://azota.vn/bao-gia/).
- **Competitors**: Azota AI essay grading (legible handwriting, Points) — [Azota docs](https://docs.azota.vn/docs/huong-dan-su-dung/thi-online/cham-bai-thi-tu-luan/); TAK12 (typed only, aimed at learners) — [TAK12](https://tak12.com/news/n/2216/ra-mat-tinh-nang-cham-chua-bai-viet-voi-ai); CoGrader (handwritten photos, LMS sync, 100k+ teachers) — [CoGrader](https://cograder.com/); Gradescope (answer groups) — [Gradescope](https://www.gradescope.com/); MagicSchool (feedback tools) — [SolidAITech](https://www.solidaitech.com/2026/09/magicschool-ai-review-teachers-pricing-safety.html).
- **Accuracy evidence**: LLM–human QWK below 0.30 on essays — [arXiv 2603.23714](https://arxiv.org/html/2603.23714v1); human-in-the-loop handwritten grading saves about 23% of time — [arXiv 2603.13083](https://arxiv.org/html/2603.13083).

### Inferences
**Target users and payer**
- Users: Literature teachers in lower and upper secondary (later English, History, Civics), plus heads of subject groups and vice-principals.
- Payers, in order: (1) schools, through organisation licences priced per student or per teacher; (2) individual teachers on freemium; (3) Departments of Education later.
- Parents receive reports but are not the main payer, because of the new free-tuition environment and the conflict with Circular 29's spirit.

**Gap against competitors**
- Azota grades. Nobody I found runs the **post-grading loop**:
  - error taxonomy per student;
  - remediation groups that respect the free in-school session limits;
  - generated practice, re-test and progress tracking;
  - messages to parents;
  - draft comments for digital school records.
- Messy handwriting, which Azota explicitly excludes, is a second gap.

**Agentic workflow (multi-agent, human-in-the-loop)**
1. **Intake agent**: the teacher photographs or scans a stack of papers. The agent splits and orders pages, reads the student ID (a QR sticker or the roster) and flags missing pages.
2. **Transcription agent**: a vision-language model or OCR turns Vietnamese handwriting into text with confidence scores for each line, and asks for re-scans of low-confidence regions.
3. **Rubric agent**: converts MOET or school answer guides into a structured rubric (the 2025 three-component rubric plus error deductions). The teacher approves it.
4. **Grader agent**: grades several times and checks agreement. It first calibrates on 5–10 essays the teacher has graded, then shows a draft score with quoted evidence for each criterion.
5. **Moderator agent**: sends uncertain or outlier essays to the teacher's review queue. The teacher approves or edits, and the agent learns from the edits (per-teacher memory).
6. **Remediation planner**: clusters students by error type (argument structure, evidence use, spelling and punctuation). It proposes free in-school sessions within the 2-sessions-per-week cap and only for eligible groups, and books them in the school calendar after approval.
7. **Practice generator**: writes targeted micro-exercises and a re-test prompt, assigns them through the LMS or Classroom, then auto-checks and re-scores.
8. **Parent-communication agent**: drafts Zalo Official Account messages with progress and next steps. The teacher approves before sending.
9. **Records agent**: drafts per-student comments for digital school records and exports CSV to the school's management software through a vendor partner.
- **Memory**: an error profile for each student across semesters, kept for longitudinal tracking.

**MVP scope (about 7 weeks, Round 2)**
- Weeks 1–2: collect 200–300 consented, anonymised essays with teacher grades from 2–4 classes; build rubric templates for the 2025 format.
- Weeks 3–4: transcription pipeline, grader and moderator with a teacher review interface.
- Week 5: remediation planner and practice generator; Zalo OA sandbox messages.
- Weeks 6–7: live pilot with 2–3 teachers during end-of-semester-1 tests (December–January; timing inferred, not verified).
- KPIs: minutes per essay, teacher edit rate, QWK against the teacher, and how many students re-tested.

**Pilot access near HCMC**
- TDTU runs a secondary school, the **Vietnam–Finland International School (VFIS)** — [TDTU](https://www.tdtu.edu.vn/). This is a natural pilot site, pending approval.
- HCMC is a pilot Department for digital school records — [Thiếu niên](https://thieunien.vn/pha-t-ha-nh-4-trie-u-ho-so-ho-c-ba-so-tbd67966.html).
