## Q1. Tourism demand (2025 and Jan–Sep 2026), policy drivers, upcoming events and traveller pain points

### Takeaway
Vietnam set a record of about 21.2M international arrivals in 2025 (+20.4%). Jan–Sep 2026 brought 17.68M (+14.5%, about 71% of the 25M target). HCMC is the largest earner: 8.52M international arrivals and VND 282.7 trillion of revenue in 9M 2026 (+43.4%). Over the same period, HCMC's domestic visitors fell 5.2%, so growth is driven by foreign guests.

Several legal and policy triggers landed between Dec 2025 and Aug 2026, which gives a strong "why now":
- Decree 282/2025 fines for undeclared foreign guests
- Abolition of the lump-sum tax
- The AI Law
- HCMC Decision 19/2026 on condo short-term rentals
- A mandatory unified residence-declaration platform from 21 May 2026
- Politburo Resolution 26-NQ/TW

APEC 2027 in Phú Quốc is the headline upcoming event. Evidence of traveller pain is mostly incident-based: overcharging, scams, language disputes and payment friction.

### Inferences
- **Growth is driven by foreign visitors.** HCMC international arrivals rose 34.5% while domestic visitors fell 5.2%. F&B consumers also say they will cut spending (Q3). Products that make money from **serving foreign guests** have a tailwind; plays built on domestic consumers have a headwind.
- **About 45% of 2025 arrivals came from China and Korea** (5.28M + 4.33M of 21.17M). That makes Chinese and Korean messaging, and channels like WeChat, KakaoTalk and LINE, a concrete differentiator for small businesses that currently work only in Vietnamese and English on Zalo.
- **Cross-border QR covers only about 6% of merchants** (154k of 2.5M). Payment friction will continue for small vendors. An agent can send VietQR payment links or point tourists to merchants that accept QR.
- **The Dec 2025 – Aug 2026 run of legal changes is unusually strong pitch material:** Decree 282, then the tax reform, the AI Law, HCMC Decision 19, the unified declaration platform and Resolution 26. Each has an exact date that judges can verify.
- **APEC 2027 and the HCMC MICE push are useful narrative hooks** but bring little direct revenue to HCMC small businesses in 2026–27.

## Q2. Accommodation SMEs: scale, OTA dependence, staffing, messaging, pricing, reviews, admin obligations; Vietnamese PMS/channel managers and global AI hospitality agents

### Takeaway
About 40,000 establishments (about 800,000 rooms) make up a long tail of small properties. In HCMC, four- and five-star hotels are under 2% of the roughly 4,800 establishments.

These small properties face four pains:
- **OTA commissions** of 15–25%
- **A staffing crisis:** 89.7% of HCMC units are hiring and 71.8% find it hard
- **Tougher compliance:** fines of VND 3–20M for undeclared foreign guests (Decree 282/2025, from 15 Dec 2025), plus a new mandatory declaration platform since 21 May 2026
- **Growing numbers of Chinese and Korean guests**

Local PMS products are cheap (from VND 40k per room or VND 180k per property per month) and mostly offer no AI. Vietnamese AI players focus on large chains or voice receptionists. Global agents prove the category and its price points (HiJiffy, Canary, Conduit, Visito, Guesty), but they lack Zalo, Vietnamese compliance and VietQR.

Resolution 26's target of about 80,000 new rooms a year, plus condo hosts newly legalised by Decision 19/2026, will add new properties that need compliance-ready tools.
- **ROI story (illustrative assumptions, not sourced):**
  - Assume a 20-room property, 70% occupancy, an ADR of VND 600,000, 50% of bookings through OTAs and an average commission of 18%.
  - It pays about VND 22.7M a month in commission.
  - Shifting 10% of OTA bookings to direct saves about VND 2.3M a month.
  - Avoiding a single Decree 282 fine saves at least VND 3M.
  - Either easily covers an agent priced at about VND 1M a month.
- **Compliance automation alone is not novel.** The government platform already does OCR, and ezCloud already offers passport scanning. The gap is the **orchestration around** declaration:
  - Collecting passport photos by chat before arrival

  - Reminding staff before deadlines
  - Preparing batches for one-click human submission
  - Keeping audit logs, because Decree 282 fines scale with the number of undeclared guests
- **Global agents validate both demand and price points** ($18–48 per unit per month; €99–319 per month). From the feature lists I found, none supports Zalo, Vietnamese compliance, VND/VietQR or local PMS integrations.
- **I found no SME-priced Vietnamese agent that combines** multilingual (Chinese/Korean) messaging, compliance orchestration and pricing actions. The search budget limited this scan.
- **Staff shortages are worst in housekeeping and front desk.** An agent can absorb front-desk and night-shift messaging and dispatch housekeeping tasks. It cannot do the physical work.

## Q5. Evaluation of candidate Agentic AI ideas, scores, ranked shortlist and saturated ideas to avoid

### Takeaway
**Best fit: Idea A, an "Agentic Front Desk + Compliance + Revenue copilot" for homestays, serviced apartments and hotels of 50 rooms or fewer (31/40).** It has:
- The strongest dated "why now": Decree 282/2025 fines; the mandatory MPS platform from 21 May 2026; HCMC Decision 19/2026; the 89.7% hiring need; record foreign arrivals
- A deep multi-agent design
- A feasible 7-week MVP with pilots in HCMC

To win the student vote, add a light guest-facing "in-stay concierge" (the useful part of Idea B) and an "agent-ready direct booking" module (Variant F).

Runners-up are Idea C, a tour-operator ops agent (29/40), and Idea D, an F&B back-office agent (29/40). A standalone tourist companion (Idea B, 24/40) is crowded and hard to monetise.

**Competitors and the gap**

  1. Messaging guests on Zalo, WhatsApp, WeChat and KakaoTalk in Vietnamese, English, Chinese and Korean
  2. Orchestrating compliance for the MPS platform
  3. Proposing rates and, once approved, pushing them out
  4. Dispatching housekeeping tasks and handling reviews
  5. Taking deposits through VietQR

  This is an inference; the competitor scan was limited.

**Agentic workflow** (orchestrator plus 5 agents, with shared memory of guest profiles, property policies and history)
1. **Guest-Comms Agent**
   - Monitors WhatsApp, Zalo OA, web chat and email. OTA messages arrive through the channel manager or email relay where possible.
   - Replies in the guest's language using the property's knowledge base and booking data.
   - Actions: holds or modifies bookings, sends VietQR deposit or extras links, schedules airport pickups, books partner tours.
   - Escalates to the owner when its confidence is low.
2. **Compliance Agent**
   - When a booking is confirmed, asks the guest for passport or CCCD photos in chat.
   - Runs OCR and checks fields: name, nationality, document number, and entry and stay dates.
   - Chases missing items and prepares the declaration batch for tbltkbtt.bocongan.gov.vn.
   - Reminds staff before the deadline and keeps an audit log.
   - **A human makes the final submission.** No public API was found, so the agent does not automate the government portal.
3. **Revenue Agent**
   - Daily inputs: competitor rates (public listings or a curated list), an events calendar and booking pace.
   - Proposes rate and minimum-stay changes and sends them to the owner on Zalo.
   - On approval, pushes them through the channel-manager API (if one is available) or outputs a change list.
4. **Reputation Agent**
   - Pulls new reviews (Google Business Profile; OTA reviews pasted in or relayed).
   - Drafts replies in the reviewer's language. Positive replies post automatically; negative ones go to the owner, following Guesty's approach.
5. **Ops Agent**
   - Turns guest issues into housekeeping or maintenance tasks and sends them to Zalo groups.
   - Sends the owner a daily brief: occupancy, ADR, arrivals, pending declarations.

Suggested tooling: an LLM with function calling, OCR/vision, a scheduler, WhatsApp Cloud API, Zalo OA API, Google Sheets or PMS connectors, a vector store for memory, and n8n or LangGraph for orchestration. Guardrails:
- Tell guests they are talking to an AI, as the AI Law requires.
- Require approval for any action involving money, prices or legal matters.
- Encrypt personal data and keep it only as long as needed.

**MVP scope (about 7 weeks, Round 2, 23 Nov 2026 – 10 Jan 2027)**
- W1: recruit 3–5 pilot properties; load their policies into the knowledge base; set KPIs.
- W2–3: build the Guest-Comms Agent on WhatsApp, Zalo OA (or Telegram as a fallback) and a web widget, in Vietnamese, English, Chinese and Korean. Bookings come from a Google Sheet or PMS export; the owner approves from an inbox.
- W3–4: build the Compliance Agent: capture in chat, OCR, validation, export in the platform's input format, reminders and the audit log.
- W4–5: add the Revenue Agent (suggestions plus approval) and Reputation Agent drafts.
- W5–6: add Ops Agent tasks over Zalo and the owner dashboard.
- W6–7: run the pilot in the year-end season. Measure first-response time, the share of conversations resolved without a human, declarations prepared on time, owner hours saved and upsell revenue. Use the data for the poster and video.

**Pilot access near HCMC** (suggestions; no relationships verified)
- Hostels and homestays in the District 1 backpacker area
- Serviced apartments in central districts and Thủ Đức
- Vũng Tàu guesthouses, now within HCMC after the merger
- Condo hosts registering under Decision 19/2026
- Possible channels: PMS vendors (ezCloud is both competitor and potential integration partner), hospitality job platforms, the team's own network

**Revenue model and pricing benchmarks**
- Tiered SaaS, all prices assumptions:
  - Starter (up to 10 rooms): about VND 399k/month
  - Pro (up to 30 rooms): about VND 990k/month
  - Business (up to 50 rooms or multi-property): about VND 1.99M/month
- Plus 5–10% commission on upsells the agent sells, and a setup fee.
- Benchmarks: ezCloud VND 180k/month; GoHost VND 40k per room/month; neoHotel VND 2.99–7.99M/month; HiJiffy €99–319/month; Conduit $18–48 per unit/month; Visito $99+/month.
- ROI pitch: one avoided Decree 282 fine (at least VND 3M), or shifting 10% of OTA bookings to direct (about VND 2.3M a month in the illustrative case), more than pays for the subscription.

**Key risks and mitigations**

| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| OTA and MPS integration limits | Integrate through channel managers; keep government submission human |
| Incumbents (ezCloud, GoHost) bundle AI | Position as an agent layer that works with their PMS, not a replacement |
| Handling passport data (sensitive personal data) | Collect consent, encrypt, minimise retention |
| LLM mistakes on prices or policies | Require human approval for anything involving money or legal matters |
| Small businesses are unwilling to pay and churn | Pitch on ROI; price per property, not per seat |
| AI Law transparency duties | Disclose the AI to guests in every channel |

#### Idea B — Safety and fair-price companion for foreign tourists (HCMC) — weak as a standalone product

**Target users and payer**
- Users: independent foreign tourists in HCMC (8.52M international arrivals in 9M 2026), especially first-timers and older solo travellers, who are typical targets for staged scams.
- Payers, all with unproven willingness to pay:
  - Tourists on a freemium plan (low WTP)
  - B2B2C buyers: hostels and hotels bundling it for guests, travel insurers, payment providers
  - Tourism authorities as sponsors

**Pain evidence**
- Overcharging of 3×, 4×, 14× and 50× the normal price — [VnExpress Intl](https://e.vnexpress.net/news/travel/vietnam-tourism-struggles-to-prevent-overcharging-of-tourists-5046087.html)
- Staged card-game scams: 18 cases, about $10k taken — [SGGP](https://www.sggp.org.vn/bat-nhom-nguoi-nuoc-ngoai-dan-canh-lua-dao-du-khach-tai-tphcm-post863666.html) (snippet)
- Disputes over language and payment — [Tuổi Trẻ News](https://news.tuoitre.vn/foreign-tourists-refuse-to-pay-how-should-vietnamese-service-workers-respond-103260908154351133.htm) (snippet)
- Only about 6% of merchants accept cross-border QR — [Báo Chính phủ](https://baochinhphu.vn/thanh-toan-qr-cua-du-khach-quoc-te-tai-viet-nam-tang-manh-co-thi-truong-gap-8-lan-102260925184104316.htm)

**Why now**
- Record arrivals.
- Experts are publicly asking for reference-price apps.
- Cross-border QR is expanding (China, Korea, Singapore).

**Market size** (theoretical; assumes US$3 per trip)

| Level | Basis | Size |
|---|---|---|
| TAM | 25M arrivals (2026 target) × $3 | ≈ $75M |
| SAM | HCMC 11M target × $3 | ≈ $33M |
| SOM | 0.5% of HCMC arrivals ≈ 55,000 payers × $3 | ≈ $165k/yr |
| B2B2C alternative | 500 properties × VND 200k/month × 12 | ≈ VND 1.2bn/yr |

These figures are highly uncertain.

**Competitors and the gap**
- Competitors: TripGenie (live translation and menu help), Google Maps and AI Mode, Mindtrip, Layla/Expedia, ChatGPT, Vie Travel AI (which already covers emergencies), the Visit Vietnam app, Grab's upfront fares, and city hotlines and rapid-response teams.
- Gap: no product combines **fair-price verification, scam-pattern alerts and help with disputes or complaints** for Vietnam.

**Agentic workflow**
- **Price-Check Agent:** the tourist photographs a menu or quote; the agent runs OCR, compares against a reference-price database (curated by the team, plus crowd input), and produces a polite negotiation script in Vietnamese text-to-speech.
- **Safety Agent:** location-based scam alerts, check-in timers, and an SOS flow that shares location and drafts a report to the police or hotline in Vietnamese.
- **Concierge Agent:** books Grab or restaurants through deep links and routes the tourist to merchants that accept VietQR.
- **Complaint Agent:** compiles evidence such as receipts, photos and GPS, and files it with authorities or rating channels.

**MVP**
- WhatsApp or Telegram bot plus a PWA.
- A reference-price list of about 50 items in District 1, collected by hand.
- 10 scam patterns and an emergency flow.
- Pilot with 2–3 hostels.

**Revenue**
- Freemium pass, B2B2C licences to properties, affiliate commissions (rates unverified) and sponsorship.

**Risks**
- Liability for wrong prices or safety advice.
- Defamation risk if specific vendors are named.
- Sensitive location data.
- Tourists may not install yet another app (so go WhatsApp-first).
- Big platforms could copy it quickly.

**Revenue**

**Risks**

#### Saturated ideas to avoid in this domain
1. **Generic AI itinerary or trip planners.** TripGenie (+400% AI-assisted bookings), Mindtrip, Layla (bought by Expedia), Google AI Mode, Vie Travel AI, EZTRIP AI and Travel Path all occupy this space (Q4).
2. **Hotel FAQ chatbots or "virtual receptionists" that only answer questions:** Saola AI, Vertize AI, Vpage, HiJiffy, Visito, Asksuite (Q2).
3. **AI translation or menu-reading apps for tourists.** TripGenie's live translation and menu assistance usage is up about 300% (Q4).
4. **A national or city tourism super-app or portal.** "Visit Vietnam" already exists (Q4).
5. **A new OTA or booking aggregator.** Foreign OTAs dominate (about 80% in 2022, background), and online travel GMV is US$4B (Q1, Q2).
6. **Standalone dynamic pricing for hotels:** ezCloud ezRms, neoHotel and Guesty PriceOptimizer already offer it (Q2).
7. **Building a PMS or channel manager from scratch.** ezCloud has 9,000+ properties, and GoHost, KiotViet Hotel and 15+ other products compete (Q2).
8. **QR or cross-border payments for tourists:** VietQRGlobal (NAPAS/Payoo), VNPAY-QR, Alipay and WeChat Pay links already exist (Q1).
9. **Restaurant POS or QR-menu ordering:** KiotViet, Sapo, CukCuk and iPOS cover it (Q3).
10. **Review-sentiment dashboards on their own:** Saola AI reputation management and Guesty's review drafter already do this (Q2).
11. **AR/VR virtual tours and AI audio-guide apps.** These are likely crowded, but I gathered no evidence here, so this is unverified.
